## PC.C2 — The positive controls actually instantiate the stated effects

**Claim detected:** a miss is measured against a planted, net-profitable alternative at the registered scale, rather than a cost-erased or incorrectly timed signal.

**Pass:** implement PC2's N2 construction, synthetic funding source (daily half-life, existing 168-hour block bootstrap), two planted coins and the all-12-coin weight-0.1 oracle without editing frozen source. Check formula/DSL decision streams, lag, warm-up, clipping and fill timing against a separately specified witness. A type-check alone is insufficient. If no faithful DSL implementation exists, publish the exact language/timing counterexample as “cannot express”; no extension or misleading agent-discovery claim. This blocks a full matrix exit, not the finding.

Before judged markets, calibration uses the fixed [0,0.08] bracket, two endpoints, at most 12 bisections on 20 ten-year markets, then 20 separate ten-year validation markets per effect. Validation oracle net Sharpe must lie in [0.9,1.1] and [1.8,2.2], respectively, and its across-market one-sided 99% Student lower mean bound must exceed zero. Report means, standard deviations, intervals, costs and all probes, including failures. A sampled non-increasing/unreachable bracket or failed validation blocks the cohort, never selects a stronger effect. No old beta reused. **Derivation:** PC2: target Sharpes 1 and 2, ±10% scenario tolerance, 200 years per bank; ≤600 development evaluations. The bracket and iteration count reuse the existing N2 search; 0.08/2^12 is the final beta grid width, not an effect-error guarantee. Fee 4.5 bps plus synthetic 2/4 bps bucket slippage and funding follow PC2/E1.

**False-pass attempt:** choose beta from gross Sharpe, get net-negative after turnover, or evaluate the signal one bar early. Net validation and stream/timing witness reject both. **False-fail attempt:** a true Sharpe-1 effect yields validation 0.89 by sampling. This is a real possibility, not cured by rerunning: the criterion certifies the registered measured scenario, not its unknowable population Sharpe. Failure is labelled calibration uncertainty, never “the DSL cannot express” or “agents cannot find edges.” The 10% bound remains a scenario check, not a 99% accuracy claim.

## PC.C4 — Every scientific miss has an evidenced first bottleneck

**Claim detected:** the study identifies where planted recovery was lost, not just whether some strategy was promoted.

**Pass:** the truth assay runs only after all searches/freezes, on a separate shared 20 × ten-year planted bank and its 20 paired beta-zero counterparts. All distinct evaluated trading programs are covered (≤3216, including all oracle candidates). Both net mean and paired planted-minus-zero mean must have positive one-sided lower Student endpoints for recovery; use tail 0.01/(2 × 3216) per quantity, df = 19 (critical ≈6.51). An upper endpoint ≤0 on either quantity establishes non-recovery; otherwise the candidate is unresolved. Retain intervals, assumptions, bank hashes and candidate identities. No equal-syntax-only or high-search-Sharpe definition of discovery. **Derivation:** PC3/S4's union bound; 3216 = 2 × 8 × (100 + 100 + 1); at most 128,640 ten-year replays. This is a finite, approximate truth assay, not universal verification of signs under arbitrary tails.

For every campaign record recovered evaluated set D, recovered frozen set F and recovered promoted set H, with H ⊆ F ⊆ D, and exactly one outcome:

1. Cannot express: a demonstrated language/timing obstruction, not a process failure.
2. Did not discover: faithful expression, D empty.
3. Discovered but not selected: D nonempty, F empty; identify DSR/SPA/StepM/prediction/mechanism losses.
4. Selected but not confirmed: F nonempty, H empty; identify net-return/betting/Holm/missing-result losses.
5. Recovered: H nonempty (not a miss).

All 48 primary outcomes must be supported, with zero unresolved first bottlenecks for complete-attribution exit; candidate ambiguity that cannot change the campaign's sets/outcome is still reported but need not block that outcome. Publish candidate-level attrition too. Operational/evidence failures and ambiguity that can change a campaign's outcome remain unresolved, in the denominator, with both-way rate bounds; they block this criterion, never force a false assignment to one of the four causes. No resampling to remove them.

**False-pass attempt:** an unrelated lucky strategy promotes while no planted rule was recovered, or an approximate funding strategy is called “not discovered” because its hash differs from the oracle. Paired truth gain and testing every submitted program defeat both; an unrelated promotion is separately reported. **False-fail attempt:** a different DSL expression truly exploits the plant, but its truth interval overlaps zero. Its outcome is unresolved unless another certified candidate settles the campaign. The study cannot certify the population claim from insufficient evidence; it reports partial localization, not no discovery. Residual Student-model/miscoverage risk is explicit, not hidden by calling truth a theorem.

## PC.C5 — Precision and conclusions match the replication

**Claim detected:** the report answers recovery/localization at stated coarse precision without treating null results, finalists or correlated evaluations as independent evidence.

**Pass:** six complete arm/effect cells, each n = 8 independent campaigns. Give discovery, selected-recovery and confirmed-recovery counts on all eight; pointwise two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson intervals; conditional attrition counts/intervals with their actual denominators (undefined when zero); and all PC.C4 unresolved bounds. Each unconditional interval's half total width is ≤0.35. Derive rather than assert interpretation: “usually” requires a one-sided 95% lower bound >0.5 (≥7/8), “not usually” an upper bound <0.5 (≤1/8); other counts are inconclusive. Zero of eight has upper 0.3123, not zero power. These pointwise labels are not a simultaneous conclusion about all six cells or proof of superiority.

No positive recovery threshold is required to exit. The report contrasts oracle expression/selection with G100/agent discovery and the agent's additional mechanism attrition, and gives PC2's realized μ/volatility beside S3/S4a's approximate wealth hurdle. No primary long/strong diagnostic, forced sentinel or post-result holdout extension enters the rates. State the pilot-bound/mechanism qualifications and a next decision: future redesign or explicitly implementation-only work if plausible controls are lost; no claim that ordinary edges are absent and no new null sweep justified merely by zero promotions. **Derivation:** PC4: exact inversion's worst half-width 0.3585 at n=7, 0.3430 at n=8; majority is an explicit operational choice, not the old waived 30% threshold.

**False-pass attempt:** report 800 evaluations as 800 independent recoveries or count the forced finalist as a ninth successful trial. Campaign-based denominators and sentinel exclusion reject it. **False-fail attempt:** all oracle candidates are discovered and selected but none confirms. With complete attribution it passes: the reported confirmation bottleneck is the result, predicted by the capped-bet algebra. Requiring promotions would repeat C2.6's mistake; the criterion instead requires an informative report, including inconclusive answers.

## PC.C6 — The nonempty freeze/release/accounting/report path ran

**Claim detected:** the decisive stage-3 path, previously unexercised, works on one synthetic nonempty batch, including irreversible accounting.

**Pass:** on the separate S=1 sentinel, one oracle search evaluation, exactly one nonempty committed finalist freeze, then one ordinary release/claim and a result for that frozen identity. Exactly one spent claim, one primary holdout result and 360 reported returns. Ordinary α=0.05, betting, positive-return condition and Holm are unchanged; forced nomination bypasses only search selection. A promotion is not required. Check reported-window equity normalization, boundary fills, fees/funding, returns and ledger reconciliation against an independent synthetic accounting witness at |difference| ≤ 1e−9 × max(10,000, |equity|, past peak |equity|), with discrete events/counts exact. The existing boundary-fill defect, if exposed, is a failure/qualification, not a relaxed expected value.

Commit primary promotions before diagnostics; reconstruct an identical primary report from committed rows only. A repeated release is refused, adding zero claims/results. Separate disposable synthetic-id tests kill before claim, kill after claim and omit a finalist's result: no claim permits only an unchanged batch retry; a claimed incomplete batch stays spent, absent results remain p=1/return=0 in the original family and are not completed later. **Derivation:** PC5/C3's release contract; singleton is the smallest nonempty batch, exact-once counts follow irreversibility, 360 follows PC1, numerical tolerance is the pilot's K3/I5 convention rather than new accuracy evidence. Independent tests must exercise failures, not merely inspect source.

**False-pass attempt:** an empty freeze prints a polished report, a different program supplies the result, or a forced statistical rejection is labelled a normal promotion. Nonempty identity/ledger checks and unchanged promotion algebra reject these. **False-fail attempt:** the correctly accounted sentinel has p≈1 and no promotion. It passes: the claim is path coverage, not power. Conversely a real edge with incorrect boundary accounting fails this criterion even if its promotion would be unchanged; that does not certify accounting.
