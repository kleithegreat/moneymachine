## 2. Kernel v1 as frozen

Two texts exist. The **frozen pilot engine `mm`** (Rust, `kernel/`) and Lean's stage 6 model implement `reference/SEMANTICS.md` as of `f591e3b`. The **current** `SEMANTICS.md` (revision 2026-11-04, lines 6-22) and `INVARIANTS.md` add the nine-defect fixes and an account stop-out at Λ = 4, specified but implemented in no engine (their tests are strict xfails: 6 in `gate/tests/test_kernel_defects.py`, 3 in `test_mechanism_defects.py`). Where they differ it is noted.

**Decision ticks.** Evaluation runs on an hourly clock: tick τ = H + δ, δ = 120 s (`policy.json:2`, `delta_ms`). All observations with knownAt ≤ τ are delivered first, then every expression is evaluated once (`DSL.md:40-44`; `SEMANTICS.md` §4). Decisions happen only at 4h boundaries (00, 04, … 20 UTC), at t_d = B + δ (`DSL.md:46`; `SEMANTICS.md` §7 step 9). Windows (`lag`, `mean`, …, `hours` 1 to 4380) count hourly ticks (`DSL.md:106-108`). The loop's state step is the 4h bar: BAR = 4 · HOUR is hard-wired into funding, marks, stop-out, fills and returns (`SEMANTICS.md:28-29`, §7). Run horizon: steps while b + 2·BAR ≤ E; due orders execute only if b + 3·BAR ≤ E (§7, step 7).

**Inputs.** `funding` (1/h) and `premium` from the latest settled hourly row; `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume` of the latest closed 4h bar; `forum_posts`, `gov_proposals` event counts (`DSL.md:84-89`). Funding knownAt = max(reportedTime floored to the hour + δ, reportedTime + 60 s) (`SEMANTICS.md:100-101`; `policy.json:3`); a bar's knownAt = eventTime + δ. There is no book, mid, bid, ask, trade or sub-4h price input, and no input for the strategy's own position, entry price, open orders or fills: T5 rejects `fill_price` and `mark` as `E_UNKNOWN_INPUT` (`INVARIANTS.md:49`). The only output is `weight`, a target fraction of equity per coin (`DSL.md:35`).

**Order and fill model.** One order per coin per decision, sized from target weight: qty = trunc(w · e_d / price − held) to szDecimals, price = the held bar's close; w = 0 closes in full (`SEMANTICS.md` §7 step 9). Portfolio rules: Σ|w| ≤ 2 (current text: β = max(0, 2 − H), H the gross of held-without-weight coins, K3); order notional ≤ $10,000 at the decision price; orders under $10 dropped except exact closes (`DSL.md:57-63`; `INVARIANTS.md` I9). The order is a market (taker) order submitted at H + δ and filled at the open of the first bar opening at or after submission, the bar at H + 4h; unfilled, never rolled, if that bar has no trade (`DSL.md:51-54`; `INVARIANTS.md` I3). Fill price = reference · (1 + s · bps/1e4), s = +1 buy, −1 sell, bps from `venue/hyperliquid/execution.json` per coin, side and bucket (≤ $1k or $10k; the 0.95 quantile of a taker walk-the-book cost model calibrated on recorded L2, `audit/criteria.md:36`, C0.7). No limit price, no maker fill, no queue, no partial fill. Current text only: a fill is admitted only if it reduces |q| without flipping sign or leaves gross ≤ 4 × equity (I3, I9).

**Fees.** fee = |qty| · price · taker_fee, taker_fee = 0.00045 (`INVARIANTS.md:14`; `rules.json:5`). `rules.json:11` carries `maker_fee` 0.00015, which no v1 rule reads. No volume tiers, rebates or staking discounts. Both fees are flagged "history before 2026-10-19 unknown; current value applied to all of W" (the taker base tier "was 0.035% before an undated change").

**Funding.** Settled every hour on the position held, priced at the open of the coin's latest bar opening at or before that hour, i.e. the containing 4h bar's open; paid in step 1 of the next 4h step, before marks and fills; a missing rate aborts with `R_SETTLEMENT_MISSING` (`SEMANTICS.md` §7 step 1; `DSL.md:54-55`). The venue's rule is payment = size · oracle_price · rate, capped at 4 %/h (`rules.json:23-33`): v1 substitutes the bar open for the oracle price.

**Margin and liquidation.** One cross book; no isolated margin. Checked once per 4h step at each held coin's adverse extreme of its last bar (low for longs, high for shorts). Frozen `mm`: maintenance = Σ |q| · adverse / (2 · min tier maxLeverage) (`f591e3b:reference/SEMANTICS.md:191`), mirroring the venue's 1/(2 · maxLeverage) with the coin's lowest tier (`rules.json:46-56`). Current text: maintenance factor max(1/(2 · min tier leverage), 1/Λ), Λ = 4, "the account's own stop-out, not the venue's liquidation" (`SEMANTICS.md:205-221`; `audit/decisions.md:703`). A breach closes every position at its adverse extreme and leaves every order due then unfilled. The venue's trigger is "account equity at mark < total maintenance margin (cross)", flagged "historical marks unavailable; evaluator uses each bar's adverse extreme" (`rules.json:52-56`).

**Equity floor.** A step whose equity, after margin and delisting, is not positive is a bankruptcy: positions close at the adverse extreme, the venue absorbs the shortfall (cash = 0, `absorbed`), every due order is unfilled, and every later return is 0. Step return ≥ −B with B = 1; L6 proves the `R_BOUND` check never fires (`INVARIANTS.md` I10; `SEMANTICS.md` §7 step 5-6). Accounting check I5 at every step against an independent average-entry ledger, tolerance 1e-9 · max($10,000, |equity|, peak |equity|) (`INVARIANTS.md:16, 24`).

**Delisting.** Current text: a coin is listed on [w_start, min(E, knownAt of its certified delisting record)); its position closes at the record's settlement price, else the mark. "No certified source states a delisting time yet, so a market whose rules mark a coin delisted is inadmissible" (`SEMANTICS.md:95-107, 202-224`). Frozen `mm`: eligibility start(c) ≤ B and B + 2·BAR ≤ end(c), with end inferred from trading (K1's lookahead, `f591e3b:reference/SEMANTICS.md:215`).

**Numerics and identity.** IEEE binary64; Rust's `f64::min`/`max` on ties (first argument); every sum a left fold from −0.0 (rustc 1.95); non-finite node values become Undef (`SEMANTICS.md` §1, §5). Identity I7 hashes strategy, params, universe, window, dataset version, every store file, `rules.json`, `execution.json`, conventions (δ, settlement latency, window bounds, decision grid, fill rule), kernel build hash and seed (`INVARIANTS.md:18`). The equivalence key (R v1, 16 rules, `egg` saturation) is in `kernel/CANONICAL.md`.

**Choices v2 must change for limit orders, maker fills and sub-4h timeframes:** (1) the 4h decision grid, the hourly clock and BAR as the loop's step (`DSL.md:40-49`, §7); (2) the inputs: no book, sub-4h price or own state (position, entry, resting orders), though a ladder conditions on its own fills; (3) one target weight per coin, where a ladder rests several priced orders; (4) the fill at the next 4h open with a taker cost table, with no limit price, resting life, cancel, partial fill or queue (I3); (5) the single taker fee; (6) funding at the bar open, not the oracle price; (7) margin once a step at bar extremes, cross only, with I9's gross cap of 2 and $10,000 order cap; (8) delisting admissibility; (9) I7's conventions, which name the decision grid and fill rule.

## 8. Study 2's open disputes

From `audit/program/ledger.md:128-138`, Argon's round 4 on revision 4 (`audit/reviews/2026-11-11-argon-web-rearchitecture-r4.md`); all "Waits for the kernel version 2 session". The design they attack is `audit/program/architecture.md` §2.2 (lines 32-38) and §6 (lines 246-257): the tested series is the replica's net return a 4h step minus a fixed short of its universe sized to its pre-freeze average exposure, every leg on isolated margin posted at the step's start, the step's loss bounded by the margin, which must be at most √2190/S = 23.4 step standard deviations at S = 2; wealth bets Kelly's fraction for Sharpe 2, registered from the pre-freeze replay, and the claim is wealth 20.

- **D11, margin** (finding 1, critical; r4 lines 65-273). With σ_m = 1.5 % a 4h step, average exposure 0.5 and exposure SD 0.25, the hedged step SD is 0.375 %, so 23.4 σ allows 8.77 % total margin, 4.39 % a leg (11× to 23× leverage): a 4.4 % dip liquidates the ladder and an 8.8 % rally the hedge, failing fidelity to the cross-margined vault; realistic margin (50 %) gives L = 133 σ and the gate's capped regime. Separate isolated legs make a straddle: max(−M, βr) + max(−M, −βr) ≥ 0, a positive drift under a symmetric null (+0.02 % a step in its example), breaking E[R | F] ≤ 0.
- **D12, hedge** (finding 2, critical; lines 275-417). With exposure β_t and a fixed short β̄_pre, E[R | F] = (β_t − β̄_pre) E[r_m | F] − c, so a shift in ladder utilization after the freeze turns market drift into "edge" with zero timing skill: β̄ 0.25 → 0.65 in a +80 %/yr market gives Sharpe 2.0 and 77 % two-year confirmation; the bear case works in reverse. The pre-freeze decomposition is blind by construction (its residual exposure is identically zero).
- **D13, bet** (finding 4, high; lines 574-711). The registered fraction 2/σ̂_pre over-bets when post-freeze volatility rises: with r = σ_post/σ̂_pre = 1.5 and Sharpe 1.4, log drift 2rS − 2r² = −0.30/yr and two-year power falls from 0.47 to 0.34. At the registered bet b = 2, 80 % power at Sharpe 1.4 takes 6.13 years, not 4.4 (`arithmetic.py.txt:127` assumed b = 1.4); discrete 4h steps and kurtosis cost a further 3 to 5 points.
- **D14, fidelity** (finding 5, high; lines 713-762). A ladder reverse-engineered on the same dates it is checked on absorbs kernel fill errors: a fill model overstating passive fills by 25 % is matched by thresholds 15 to 30 bps wider, passing the daily-return wall. 5 s snapshots miss sub-5 s liquidation wicks that fill dip ladders, and the cross-margined vault cannot validate a 4h isolated-margin series (or the reverse). Its remedies: replay the vault's own order and trade events, or a held-out pre-freeze sub-window.
- **D15, perp brackets** (finding 7, low; lines 863-1003). The account's proof needs the all-in cost c known at registration; a fill priced at the next snapshot must be indexed at fill time, else Jensen's wedge E[x/c] ≠ E[x]/E[c]. A perp bracket fits x ∈ [0, 1] only with isolated margin equal to the stake and funding deducted; `arithmetic.py.txt:81` counts pending payouts as cash at the horizon (0.0235 → 0.0242). No study uses the account.

## 10. Questions the facts leave open

1. **Fill evidence for resting orders.** The archive has books, not trades, at 0.54 s to 5.4 s; the only archived trades are in the uncertified `hl-mainnet-node-data`. Certify it (within the $17.54 left), or define maker fills from snapshots alone and bound what D14's wicks cost?
2. **Clock.** Cycles last a median 1.16 hours and 10 % under 6 minutes, while the archive's cadence changes tenfold on 2026-06-21. Does v2 step on every snapshot, on a fixed grid, or on events, and is one rule valid across both cadences?
3. **Margin.** The vault is cross-margined and never liquidated; the tested series asks for isolated legs bounded at 23.4 σ (D11). Which account does the kernel simulate, and where does the loss bound come from if not per-leg margin?
4. **Hedge.** Which hedge removes market drift when exposure moves with the ladder (D12): fixed, rebalanced each step, or none with a different null?
5. **Fidelity reference.** With `vaultDetails` and `portfolio` unreadable before 2027-01-31, is the reference the vault's own fills (order-level replay against the archive, D14's remedy) rather than its equity curve?
6. **Coin rules.** 12 of the 17 coins have no `szDecimals`, tiers or price tick in `rules.json`, `meta` is current-only, and the tick rule is not certified anywhere. Where do historical lot and tick sizes come from?
7. **Forward data.** The recorders cover 5 of the 17 coins at 30 s; the archive uploads monthly with no guarantee. What feeds the frozen forward test, and from when?
8. **The DSL.** A ladder needs its own position, average entry and resting orders as state. Does v2 extend the JSON AST with order-level outputs and own-state inputs, or add a second program kind?
9. **Relation to v1.** Must v2 reproduce `mm` bit for bit on the 4h taker subset (the language plan's condition, and the human's port-first order for the nine defects), or is v1 frozen as the pilot's instrument and v2 judged only by its own laws and differential?
10. **OCaml data path.** No OCaml binding exists in the pinned nixpkgs for Parquet, DuckDB or lz4. Bind DuckDB's or Arrow's C API through `ctypes`, or convert the archive once into a format OCaml reads natively?
