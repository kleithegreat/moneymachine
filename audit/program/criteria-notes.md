## Stage 8: multi-asset research

To append beneath the stage 8 criteria.

> *Revision [date], superseded before any stage 8 work was judged (`audit/program/architecture.md`, sections 2.3, 3 D and 4; `audit/decisions.md`, [the advisor's entry]).* Stage 8 does not start as written, and no C8 condition has been or will be judged under these criteria.
> - **Why.** The stage was scoped to give search long histories. For a strategy proposed by a language model, every observation before the model's knowledge cutoff plus its margin is search data, however long the history. The judged span is the time since then, which fifteen-year histories do not lengthen (paper, Section "The confirmation wall").
> - **What stands.** Every source certificate, and the recordings, which continue under integrity-only inspection. The licensing boundary stands as a rule, and its certification does not: the frozen attack suite cannot certify it against a three-query reconstruction (`audit/program/argon-paper-findings.md`, finding 12). No kernel-only aggregate crosses until a redesigned boundary has been reviewed. No class is admitted and no annex is frozen.
> - **What would revive it.** A study with a research model whose cutoff is at least two years old, after the positive control reaches its upper decision for that model and for the frontier agent, and after a confirmer has been re-derived whose stated power at that length reaches an ordinary edge (`architecture.md`, section 4, parked items). It would be pre-registered as that study's own criteria, on evidence produced after them, and never judged as C8.
> - **Owed derivations** recorded against C8 in the paper's appendix (S4a, D3) lapse with it.

## Stage 10: thesis track

To append beneath the stage 10 criteria, after the study's criteria draft (`audit/program/briefs/2-thesis-track.md`, deliverable 2) has been reviewed. That draft is the revision's text. This note is its frame.

> *Revision [date], for a new scientific identity and its new forward window only (`audit/program/architecture.md`, sections 2.5, 5 and 8).* The track restarts as a new instance: a paper account in event contracts on one CFTC-designated exchange, under that venue's certificate. No earlier version is pooled with it, and the seven earlier versions and their windows are judged, if at all, as frozen.
> - **The skill-claim floor.** "None is made before 200 resolved main-agent clusters" is replaced, for the new instance, by one pre-registered rule: [the rejection criterion]. The floor could not be reached: a component resolves only when every member has, and components chain through common markets (`audit/reviews/2026-11-09-fable-meta-review.md`, section 3; a simulation on `thesis-split`, `audit/stage10/redesign/units_sim.txt`, resolves 0 to 30 components in a year). The replacement is stricter in kind: the floor forbade an early claim and permitted none; the rule names the one statistic, its threshold and the one claim a rejection supports.
> - **The dependence unit.** The cluster graph and its bootstrap intervals are not used by the new instance. Its judged statistic is the cash of one self-financing paper account. Under the null that no purchase has positive conditional expected net return, cash reaches twenty times its start with probability at most 5%, under any dependence between contracts and any stopping rule (`architecture.md`, section 5.1). No period, mark or look enters the test.
> - **What a result may be called.** A rejection is the negation of that null for the frozen procedure, at a reference stake, on paper. The mirror account reaching twenty is the only negative result. Anything else is "not confirmed", and is never called absence of edge or a statement about reading: the instance has no ablation, no no-reading arm and no benchmark, and does not test whether reading helps (`architecture.md`, section 5.2).
> - **C10.5.** Its counts and deadlines are not exit conditions for the new instance. They judged whether sessions and batches happened, which no one could game and no claim rests on; each is a line of the report (`CLAUDE.md`, Pre-registered criteria). The 2026-11-09 02:31 UTC (2026-11-08 21:31 EST) revision's items lapse with the perp instance they were written for.
> - **C10.2.** As re-derived by the human on 2026-11-09 17:01 UTC (12:01 EST): every call has one ledger record committed before any reply byte, and the client is the agent's only channel. The transcript audit fails closed and is a report line.
> - **C10.3 and C10.4** remain met for the perp positions and scoring they judged, which the parked perp instance keeps. The new instance's account gets its own independent implementation: [the agreement criterion].

## Stages 1, 2 and 4: what a recorded pass shows

These notes follow Argon's check of the paper, and are appended only if the human accepts the recommendation in `audit/program/argon-paper-findings.md`. None changes a recorded pass or failure. Each narrows what the pass may be said to show.

> *Qualification [date], beneath C1.7 (`argon-paper-findings.md`, finding 14).* C1.7 stands as met under its amendment. It detects gross lookahead only: over 550 days a leak worth an annualized Sharpe of 2 stays below the alarm at 4 in 99% of single runs. It is not a certificate that the evaluator has no leak. A study that rests on the evaluator runs its own check (the positive control's third model-free check).

> *Qualification [date], beneath C2.5 (finding 6).* C2.5 stands as met. Its statistic counts a campaign with no promotion as a false-discovery proportion of zero, so a cell that promotes in at most 7 of 100 campaigns meets it whatever it promotes. The two sensitivity cells the paper reports promoted in none of 100 campaigns and in 5. C2.5 therefore shows that few campaigns promoted, and nothing about how reliable a promotion is.

> *Qualification [date], beneath the C2 holdout revisions (finding 10).* The v3 locked suite stands as passed. Its tolerances were kept from the tests it replaced, and an implementation whose true rejection rate is 0.0075 at level 0.005 passes them 65% of the time (`arithmetic.py.txt`). It is an implementation check with that resolution. The test's level rests on the proof and on the return bound, which is not established for the frozen pilot or for real markets.

> *Qualification [date], beneath C4.5 (finding 4).* C4.5 v3 stands as passed as written. The paper's pooled bound over v1, v2 and v3 (0.133) is withdrawn: three versions stopped and redesigned in turn are not one procedure sampled 22 times. The counts stand as counts. V3 alone, no promotion in ten campaigns, bounds its promotion rate at 26%.
