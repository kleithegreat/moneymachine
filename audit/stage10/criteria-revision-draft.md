- **Outages and missed sessions:** an absent snapshot at 19:00, or a completion timestamp later than 19:00, consumes main and no-reading as missed, with the acquisition-deadline reason. Do not fetch a late replacement or reuse another date's snapshot. Recovery requires both the timely completion record and those exact published bytes; missing or changed bytes fail closed. Publication without a completion record can be attested only before the deadline. A model/canary outage waits only until the UTC date ends; disclose all remaining unstarted arms as missed then. A crash consumes the started session and records its end on recovery; remaining arms retain main's snapshot. SIGTERM leaves unfinished work to restart, without backdating it. Larger inventories, extra holes, retries or foreground contention may cause a miss, never relaxed age/readiness or eligibility.

Otherwise raise the same availability failure as an unsuccessful input read; commit no thesis/comparator. Main/no-reading receive the existing failed-call disclosure; the benchmark retries within its Monday or records the existing missed-batch skip. Acquired-but-omitted rows still follow the existing complete-window rule. Eligibility itself is unchanged.

## Why this time, and what it does not promise

The derivation is in **`docs/whitepaper.tex`, appendix T3** (`app:thesis-policy`), not a tail-minute offset from 00:10. Under ceiling rounding, 524 tails cost 11,004 weight (91.7 ideal minutes, 105 rolling batches). Actual calls also include discovery, prefix-omission probes and possible terminal empty requests: at the recorded inventory and d <= 5, the no-interior-hole envelope is **1,509 calls / 50,590 weight**. An order-independent packing bound is 701 waiting minutes. A chosen transient/service envelope allows five extra attempts, 1,514 × 10 seconds total non-admission work (HTTP, validation, publication and completion bookkeeping), 31 seconds backoff, seven minutes per hour of mark interference, and two debt/scheduler minutes. Its bound is **65,631 seconds = 18:13:51**; cutoff plus bound is **18:18:51**, so the first whole-hour start after completion is **19:00**. Five calendar hours remain for the at-most-four one-hour sessions.

The ten-second service allowance is **not** HTTPX's whole-request timeout. Other foreground work (notably Monday benchmark acquisition and resolutions), more than five dexes, extra listings/interior holes, old tails, slow receipts or additional retries are outside this conditional bound. Six attempts on every call do not fit a daily guarantee. Actual timely completion is the admission check; a missed deadline is reported as failure, not a pass of the envelope. The former “about 45/min fits everything” claim is withdrawn. This proposal fixes the snapshot wall, not shared-IP coordination, initial-wave mark capacity or unbounded account-read latency.
