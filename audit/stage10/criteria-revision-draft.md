## The revision states

1. **The schedule.** Information cutoff D + 00:05 UTC (19:05 EST the day before). Acquisition starts at the cutoff. The completion deadline and the earliest session start are D + 05:00 UTC (00:00 EST). It is a choice from the recorded 2026-10-28 inventory, not a bound. With the venue's recorded behaviour (the unclosed candle returned in 514 of 524 scopes) the snapshot packs in 155 minutes plus two mark windows (01:00 and 02:00 UTC (20:00 and 21:00 EST)) of 7 minutes: complete by 02:54 UTC (21:54 EST), 2 h 6 min before the start. Were the unclosed candle omitted, it is 252 minutes plus four windows: 04:45 UTC (23:45 EST), a 15-minute margin (T3). The margin also covers extra attempts, ten-dex discovery and the unmeasured interior hourly holes and midnight resolution reads. A larger inventory, holes, retries or outages may cause a miss, which is reported and never relaxed.
2. **Misses.** A late or missing attested snapshot makes main and no-reading missed for the date, with that reason. There is no late snapshot and no other date's snapshot; recovery uses only the attested bytes, and changed or lost bytes fail closed. A snapshot is on time only if attested strictly before 05:00 UTC (00:00 EST); one comparison decides, and the miss decision waits for an attestation in flight. Each date's `taken`, `acquired`, `listed_at` and request count are recorded in its attestation and listed in the C10.6 report, so the miss distribution is measured; changing the start after seeing it is a new identity. The tolerated misses restate C10.5 (T3): at most 28 - 20/r main sessions, r the mean accepted theses per on-time main session. The window holds exactly four Mondays and C10.5 needs four executed benchmark batches, so a Monday batch of five skips for want of a snapshot does not count as executed (that would loosen C10.5, which only the human may do); item 5 gives the benchmark the whole date instead.
3. **Acquisition is independent of the sessions and of model readiness.** It has its own loop. Sessions may wait for the model and the sandbox canary until the UTC date ends, and are then disclosed as missed. A crash consumes the started session; SIGTERM leaves unfinished work to the next start without backdating it.
4. **Readiness.** History the venue was never asked for, or more than one page (500 hours) before the registration, makes a market unavailable for that session (the human's decision). The hours since the last fill are fetched by the registration's own read and are not unavailability. An unavailable market raises the failed-read disclosure and commits nothing; the benchmark retries within its day or records the missed-batch skip. Eligibility is unchanged.
5. **The benchmark** reads that Monday's attested snapshot, kept to the daily bars known at Monday 00:00 UTC (Sunday 19:00 EST), so it takes no second snapshot. It waits for the snapshot until the UTC date ends (a Monday's snapshot may be attested after 05:00 UTC (00:00 EST), serving the benchmark only; main and no-reading still miss) and registers at the first whole hour at or after max(05:00 UTC (00:00 EST), the attestation). So its registration hour can move later, up to 23:00 UTC (18:00 EST), while its information set cannot: the bars known at Monday 00:00 UTC (Sunday 19:00 EST), as the frozen rule defines the 20-day direction, whatever the hour. A snapshot attested after 23:00 UTC (18:00 EST) leaves the batch run missed (the first whole hour is the next date's), each market recorded as skipped "run missed"; a snapshot missing for the whole date, "no attested snapshot". Neither counts as an executed batch.
6. **The listing read time** is recorded in the snapshot (`listed_at`). After a restart acquisition may begin hours after the cutoff, and a market delisted meanwhile is dropped from a snapshot labelled 00:05; registration re-reads the listing at commit.
7. **What it changes.** `markets` shows the last 1h close as of 00:05 UTC (19:05 EST); main's entry is near 06:00 UTC (01:00 EST), against about 01:00 UTC (20:00 EST) in the 00:10 design, and HIP-3 equity perps are then in US cash hours. Daily bars and base-rate anchors are unchanged; the snapshot age at start is 4 h 55 min on time and strictly under 23 h 55 min. The one-hour, 100-turn, two-registration limits, the conditional ablations and the no-reading arm are unchanged. It is a new identity and window, never a pass of the 00:10 rule.

8. **Dex mark send times** (restates stage 10's sampling clause, "a request is sent at H + 5 s", as what the code can guarantee). At 120 weight per minute, discovery plus a cohort holds at most five dexes: the first five (lexical order) are sent at H + 5 s, each later cohort of five as the rolling minute admits it, about H + 65 s for dexes six to ten, each send time recorded in the archive; a sample counts if sent in [H, H + 5 min). Derivation in T3. **Known limitation, not a pass:** with ten dexes (the 2026-10-28 listings), the running version and this one miss H + 5 s for dexes six to ten every hour. Marks are unaffected (the samples fall inside [H, H + 5 min)); only the stated send time fails.
