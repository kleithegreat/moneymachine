# Stage 10 redesign: build plan for Sonnet

Written 2026-11-10 by the architect (Claude Fable) on `thesis-split` (cut from `thesis-refreeze` at `9719be0`). Design: `audit/stage10/criteria-revision-round11-draft.md` (sections A to D), paper `sec:thesis` and appendix T (T1 to T3), `thesis/SCORING.md`. Round fifteen (the fail-closed transcript audit, one ledger record per call with the call carried on its effect row, `fetch` storing its body) is merged (`af3627c`); its retry cutoff at 04:45 UTC (23:45 EST) goes with the acquisition it belonged to (step 6).

Rules for every step: start from a failing test named for what it holds; commit when it passes; no file covered by `gate/agent/harness.py`'s `harness_hash()` changes (`sandbox/session.py`, `gate/agent/*`; the hash stays `6a335714…`); `ruff format`, `ruff check` and `mypy --strict` on every touched Python file; the full suite through `python3 ops/shards.py <worktree>` before the last commit. Erasure is part of each step: when a function's last caller goes, the function goes in the same commit, with its tests. Scientific steps change files the manifest hashes (section A); operational steps do not.

## Before the freeze

1. **Scoring, scientific.** `thesis/scoring.py` implements the revised `SCORING.md`: `units` (registration week) and `paired` (n_pairs, mean_d, W, se, t) replace `clusters` and `bootstrap`; `COMPARISONS`, `REPLICATES`, `SEED` go; `base_rate` is unchanged. Failing test: `thesis/tests/test_scoring_units.py`, hand-derived sets (two theses in one week, two weeks, W < 2 → se null, se = 0 → t null, size-zero theses in units). `thesis/tests/scoring_gen.py` loses `session` and `catalyst_id` and covers the new boundary cases (a week with one pair, pairs across the week boundary at exactly MONDAY + k·WEEK). `test_scoring_differential.py` is skipped with a reason until step 13 and never deleted.
2. **Arms and bounds, scientific.** `thesis/harness.py`: `BRIEFS = {"main", "ablation"}`; `brief-noread.md` deleted. `thesis/daemon.py`: `MAX_REGISTRATIONS = 4`; the command lists for main (`markets`, `prices`, `fetch`, `register`, `positions`, `exit`) and the ablation (`prices`, `forecast`) move here from the runner. `thesis/registry.py`: T within [t_r + 72 h, t_r + 28 days]; `register` loses `p_is_base_rate`, `forecast_at`, `listings_at` and the base-rate computation (p_ref, `base_windows`, `base_positive` leave the record). `positions.py` takes `INITIAL`. `brief.md`: at most 4 theses, 72 hours to 28 days, `markets` as step 5 defines it. Failing tests in `test_registry.py`: T at 28 days + 1 ms refused, the fifth registration of a session refused, a record without `p_ref`.
3. **Ablation parity, scientific.** `ablation_task` moves from the runner to `thesis/harness.py` and builds the task from the thesis and from main's recorded `markets` reply of that session: the same row for each leg's market, daily bars completed before t_r, nothing of the catalyst or sources. Failing test: `test_harness.py::test_the_ablation_sees_what_main_saw_and_nothing_else`.
4. **Mark bars, scientific.** `seal` and `mark_bars` move from `market.py` to `positions.py`, unchanged; their tests move with them (`test_mark_cohorts.py` keeps the sampling cohorts, `test_positions.py` the bars). Failing test: the C10.3 mark fixtures imported from the new place.
5. **`markets` and lazy reads, scientific and operational.** The daemon's `markets` reply is each listed, non-delisted market with its listing status, the number of completed daily bars in the cache and the date and close of the last cached daily bar: no venue request beyond the day's listing read. `prices MARKET` reads that market's daily bars through the cache (one request when stale). Registration reads the requested markets' listings and daily bars, and the account's hourly bars and funding from its earliest registration, all lazily. The `revision` disclosure: `Market._cached` and the funding commit record a fetched final row that differs from the cached one (market, kind, time, both hashes) and keep the cached one. Failing tests: `test_daemon.py::test_markets_costs_no_venue_request`, `test_market.py::test_a_differing_refetch_is_recorded_not_applied`, `test_registry.py::test_registration_reads_only_its_own_markets`.
6. **The snapshot machine and readiness go, operational.** Delete from `runner.py`: `Track.snapshot`, `draft`, `draft_path`, `draft_of`, `save_draft`, `list_pass`, `read_pass`, `read_market`, `attested`, `acquire`, `acquisitions`, `attestation`, `scope_of`, `inputs`' cold check and `readiness_at`, `SNAPSHOT_OFFSET`, `RETRY_UNTIL`, `fill`, `cold`, `warm_order`, `Track.prewarm`, `prewarm()`, `PREWARM_EVERY`, `joined`'s snapshot use; from `market.py`: `ready`, `_acquired_to`, `prewarm`, `FRESH`, the `background` path of `funding` and `_funding_fill`, `MAINTAIN_EVERY`, `funding_repaired`'s background key, `hold`'s cancel, `listings_at`; from `cli.py` and `ops/live.py`: `thesis-prewarm`. `Track.ready(date)` keeps only the model and canary wait. The cache's directory layout is unchanged, so the prewarm Job's funding files stay usable. Failing test: `test_runner.py::test_a_session_starts_without_any_acquisition`; then the tests that no longer import are deleted (step 9).
7. **The benchmark goes, operational.** Delete `benchmark`, `register_benchmark`, `benchmarks`, `unbenchmarked`, `unbenchmarkable`, `known_at`, `BENCHMARK`, `BENCHMARK_GIVE_UP`, `is_monday`, `last_acquisition`, `ACCOUNTS` (the one account is `main`; ablations are records), the report's `benchmark_skips` and its benchmark statement, `NOREAD_COMMANDS`. Failing test: `test_runner.py::test_a_monday_runs_main_and_its_ablations_only`.
8. **The schedule, operational.** `Track.run`: at 05:00 UTC (00:00 EST) each day, `day(date)` runs main once the model and canary answer and the date has not ended, then each accepted thesis's ablation; the resolution loop, `deadlines`, the hourly mark sampler and a daily listing observation (`Market.observe`, the point-in-time record) continue as now; `schedule` loses its snapshot and benchmark branches. Failing test: `test_runner.py::test_a_day_runs_main_then_its_ablations_and_nothing_else`, and the kill tests in `test_crash_recovery.py` still pass.
9. **Tests named for rounds go, operational.** Delete `thesis/tests/test_refreeze_round4.py` to `test_refreeze_round15.py`, `test_refreeze_t3.py`, `test_snapshot_integrity.py`, `test_snapshot_schedule.py`, `test_readiness_tail.py`, `test_funding_fill.py`. 10. **The manifest, scientific.** `harness.manifest_files` is the explicit list of section A; `manifest_hash`'s values are unchanged. `freeze()` keeps only the canary and the "already frozen" check and prints the record with `"operational": {"commit", "image"}`. Failing tests: `test_harness.py::test_the_manifest_ignores_the_operational_layer` (editing `runner.py` leaves the hash; editing `registry.py` moves it) and `test_harness.py::test_scientific_modules_import_nothing_operational` (every listed module's imports are listed modules, `ingest.terms`, the standard library or third-party packages).
11. **The operational commit, operational.** `ops/live.py push` writes the commit into the image; `apply` passes the digest as an environment variable; the runner reads both at start and writes `operational` on every `session` and `session_end` row. Failing tests: `test_runner.py::test_sessions_carry_the_operational_commit_and_image`; `ops/test_live.py` for the variables.
12. **The report, operational.** `report.write` lists: sessions with their operational pair, transcript audit result and `markets` count; misses with reasons; registrations, accepted, size-zero; ablations completed and missing with reasons; resolutions and latencies; revisions; units and `paired` with se / se_iid; the base-rate comparison as descriptive; positions as now; distinct operational pairs with first and last session. Failing test: `test_report.py` on a hand-built ledger.
13. **C10.4 rerun, independent, not Sonnet.** GPT (or Argon while GPT's week is spent) updates `thesis/tests/gpt_scoring.py` from the revised `SCORING.md` and the diff alone and the differential runs on 1,000 sets from the new generator; `test_scoring_differential.py` loses its skip. A disagreement is resolved by a dated revision of `SCORING.md` before the freeze, as the frozen C10.4 requires. This gates the freeze and waits on the outside models' quotas (about 2026-11-13).
14. **Suite, decisions, PROGRESS.** `python3 ops/shards.py <worktree>`; one decisions line for the operational deletions (steps 6 to 9, 11, 12), citing this plan; the advisor's wake-up for the freeze; PROGRESS.md updated by the builder.
