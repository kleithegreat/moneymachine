## A. The identity split (the human, item 1)

**Scientific manifest.** The track's version is the sha256 (`sandbox.session.identity`) of exactly these, and nothing else:

- values: the model id, the turn and wall limits, `session.PROMPT`, `session.tools("thesis-agent")`, the `claude` build's store path, and the sorted retrieval allowlist (`ingest.terms.allowlist(retrieval=True)`).

That covers the model, the briefs (with the bounds they state: at most 4 theses a session, horizons 72 h to 28 days, p in [0.01, 0.99], L in [0.01, 0.5]), the tools the agent has, the limits, registry validation and eligibility, sizing (`positions.admit`, the initial equity), the price, mark-bar and outcome rules, the comparator and the unit (`SCORING.md`, `scoring.py`), what `fetch` may return (the allowlist, byte and redirect limits) and the sandbox. The import closure is no longer hashed: the manifest is this list, and a test fails if a listed module imports anything outside the list other than `ingest.terms` (the allowlist read), the standard library and third-party packages, so operational code cannot reach the scientific layer unhashed.

**Operational layer.** Everything else the track runs: `thesis/runner.py` (scheduling, sessions, resolution, misses), `thesis/market.py` (the cache, the limiter, mark sampling), `thesis/report.py`, `ingest/hl.py`, `ingest/live.py`, `sandbox/canary.py`, `ops/live.py`, the images and the cluster objects. A change there is an operational patch: a decisions line naming the failure it fixes, a targeted test, no new version, the window continuing. A change to a hashed file or value is a new scientific version: a new manifest record and a new protocol window; versions are never pooled.

**The recorded operational commit.** The image carries the commit it was built from; `ops/live.py apply` passes the digest. The runner reads both at start, and every `session` and `session_end` ledger row carries `operational: {"commit", "image"}`. The C10.6 report lists the distinct pairs with their first and last session. `audit/stage10/manifest.json` gains `"operational"` in each new version record as frozen, and is otherwise untouched by operational patches, whose record is their decisions lines.

**The operational layer is not inert.** An acquisition failure (a market the cache cannot serve, a builder dex whose listing failed) changes which markets a session's `markets` shows, so it changes which theses exist. Every `markets` reply is a recorded disclosure, so the universe each session saw is on the ledger, and the report gives, per session, the count shown and the operational commit. The protocol below is paired within a thesis, so this changes the population of theses, not the comparison inside any pair; the report states it, and the protocol's claim is conditional on the theses registered.

**The freeze.** `thesis-freeze` requires only that the sandbox canary passes and the manifest differs from the last frozen one. It no longer waits for funding readiness or a Monday listing observation (neither exists in the redesign, section C).

## C. The redesigned track, stated for the record (not criteria)

- **Arms.** Main (reads through `fetch`; registers at most 4 theses a session) and, for each accepted main thesis, its conditional ablation: the same model with no reading and the same market data, given the thesis's exposure, horizon and outcome definition, its markets' daily bars completed before t_r and the same `markets` row main saw for them, and nothing of main's catalyst text or sources. The full no-reading agent and the Monday benchmark are deleted (paper T2 says why).
- **Schedule.** One main session a day at 05:00 UTC (00:00 EST), its ablations after it; no acquisition deadline and no snapshot. Data is read when a command asks, from a cache of immutable history (completed daily bars, settled funding, closed hourly candles) filled on demand and never overwritten; builder marks and listings are recorded point-in-time, as the only data that cannot be refetched. A row fetched after it was final and found to differ from the cached one is a `revision` disclosure (market, kind, time, both hashes); the first final value stands. A market the venue stops serving leaves its theses unresolved under `SCORING.md` and is reported.
- **Base rate.** p_ref is the scorer's output (`SCORING.md`, Base rate), computed from immutable history before t_r at report time, not at registration. Registration records what sizing and scoring need: t_r, legs, T, p, L, N and the sizing amounts, the fee and cost cells, the listing read and the data cutoff.

## D. C10.7, the reading protocol (the human, item 2)

Pre-registered before any thesis of the new version is scored, committed alone, never edited; a wrong line gets a dated revision beneath it, and the protocol is then judged only on theses registered after that revision.

*Claim.* Reading through `fetch` improves the agent's probability assessment of the exposures it chose: the expected paired Brier difference μ = E[d], d = (p − o)² − (p_ablation − o)², is negative.

1. **Estimand and sample.** μ over the pairs: every resolved main thesis of the version with a non-null p_ablation and t_r in the window. No other exclusion. Size-zero theses, theses of sessions that fail the transcript audit, theses the agent exited early (scored as registered) and theses on markets the venue stopped serving (unresolved, so not pairs, by a rule that predates them) all count as the rules say. Theses with a missing ablation are counted and their reasons reported; their count is a report line, not a condition, because an ablation runs before its outcome exists and cannot be selected on it. *Derivation:* a sample rule the operator cannot apply after seeing outcomes is the one thing here anyone could game.
2. **Unit and statistic.** The unit is the registration week (`SCORING.md`, Units); the statistic is `SCORING.md`'s t = mean_d / se with W − 1 degrees of freedom, se from week sums. *Derivation:* paper T2 and `units_sim.txt`: the week-cluster interval covers at 0.925 to 0.960 of nominal 0.95 in every cell (13 to 52 weeks, 2 or 4 theses a day, horizons to 28 or 60 days, 60 markets under a Zipf choice, a one-factor outcome model with session-shared forecast shocks), where the iid interval falls to 0.88 at 4 a day and a Bartlett HAC over weeks to 0.83; the connected components of the frozen `SCORING.md` resolve 0 to 6 units in 365 days under the same concentration (30 with 1.4 theses a day spread uniformly over 278 markets), so they cannot be the unit of any test.
3. **Window and looks.** The window opens at the first Monday 00:00 UTC (Sunday 19:00 EST) at or after the protocol's freeze and holds 52 registration weeks. Two looks, one-sided, Bonferroni: the first over pairs with t_r in weeks 1 to 26, judged once every thesis registered in them has resolved or stands unresolved at T + 28 days, rejects if t < t_{0.01}(W − 1); the second over weeks 1 to 52, judged the same way, rejects if t < t_{0.04}(W − 1). The family's one-sided size is at most 0.05. *Derivation:* a first look lets a large effect end the window at about 31 weeks without pooling across versions; Bonferroni is valid under any dependence between the looks; 0.05 is a choice. *Unsure:* the human may prefer one look at 52 weeks and α = 0.05; the final power is the same to two decimals.
4. **Power, stated, not a condition.** From `units_sim.txt` at 4 theses a day (one-sided α = 0.05, single look): for μ ≈ −0.012 (reading removes half of a 0.15-SD miscalibration), power 0.62 at 13 weeks, 0.87 at 26, 0.96 at 39, 0.99 at 52; for μ ≈ −0.029 (three quarters of a 0.2-SD one), 0.96 at 13 weeks and 1.00 from 26; for μ ≈ −0.002 (a quarter), at most 0.11 at any length. At 2 theses a day the 26-week medium power is 0.67. Under the looks above, by a normal shift of these: about 0.67 (medium) and 0.99 (large) at the first look, 0.99 (medium) at the second. A null in which reading only adds noise has μ > 0 and is never rejected. These rest on assumptions, not measurements; the report gives the realized W, n_pairs and the within-week dependence (se / se_iid).
5. **The claim's wording.** If rejected: "in this window, the agent's probabilities with reading had lower Brier than its own conditional no-reading forecasts of the same outcomes, by mean_d with the stated interval; the design holds the model, the task and the market data fixed and varies only the retrieval set, so the difference is attributable to what it read". If not rejected at the second look: "no evidence at this power". Neither says anything about skill against the base rate, which stays descriptive.

**Constructions, for the reviewer to break.**
- *Passes while the claim is false:* (i) the ablation sees less market data than main (today it sees daily closes before t_r, while main's `markets` shows the last hourly close): d < 0 without reading; section C gives the ablation the same `markets` row. (ii) The operator excludes sessions or theses after seeing outcomes: condition 1 forbids every post-hoc exclusion. (iii) A persistent regime makes outcomes of different weeks on one market dependent beyond what the week unit absorbs: the 28-day horizon cap bounds the overlap; `units_sim.txt` at 60-day horizons still covers at 0.935 to 0.951; longer persistence is a stated limitation, not a guard.
- *Fails while the claim holds:* (iv) the ablation sees main's catalyst text: reading's conclusions leak into p_ablation and d → 0; section C withholds it. (v) Few resolved weeks (misses, a long outage): W small, t_α(W − 1) large; reported, not guarded, since the window is fixed. (vi) Reading improves the mean but adds variance (a session-shared shift): the Brier penalizes the variance, so μ can be positive while reading helps on average; the claim as worded is about the Brier, so this is not a false fail. (vii) Clipping p to [0.01, 0.99] after a correct extreme forecast is a bound of the brief, inside the design.

**Unsure.** The 60-market Zipf concentration and the 7-to-28-day horizon distribution are assumptions; the recorded tracks' concentration is forward-window material the architect may not read. The advisor can give the reviewer their counts (markets per session, horizons) without prices.
