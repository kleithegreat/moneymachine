## Exact proposed wording

For (a), a failed read means an exception. A completed readiness check returning false is ordinary day-fixed cold status, not a retryable read error.

**(a) Item 2, operational skips, and item 4, readiness.** The operational reasons also include "market read failed at the snapshot". A date's first pass is complete once it has read the core venue's listing and builder-dex list and attempted every builder-dex listing, market read and readiness check in scope. Until then every pass is initial, subject only to the acquisition deadlines: strictly before D + 05:00 UTC (00:00 EST) for the agents, and on a Monday until D + 24:00 UTC (19:00 EST) for its benchmark only. A failure of the core listing or of the builder-dex list that lasts to that deadline leaves the date with no attested snapshot. After the first pass is complete, later passes retry only failed market reads, failed readiness checks and failed builder-dex listings. No such retry pass, retry read or network admission within a retry pass starts at or after D + 04:45 UTC (23:45 EST the day before). The cutoff is checked within the pass and while waiting for quota. An in-flight request may finish. Before the cutoff, an outstanding failure prevents attestation. A failure is final for the snapshot if it remains at the cutoff, if it is first observed at or after it, or if its retry was left incomplete there. Final failures and their reasons are recorded in the attested snapshot and in its attestation record, and listed in C10.6. They make only the affected market, or every market of the failing builder dex, unavailable for that date: absent from the agent and benchmark market views and daily bars, and not ready. Main and no-reading still run on the identical attested snapshot, failures included. The Monday batch skips such a market operationally ("market read failed at the snapshot: <reason>"), so that batch is not executed. One `readiness_at`, fixed when the draft is created, is used by every readiness check of that draft. Registration of a newly used market reads funding from this `readiness_at` minus 500 hours through its own cutoff, and existing positions read from their earliest registration. A completed readiness check returning false is day-fixed cold status, not a read failure.

**(b) C10.2.** The daemon's per-session call records are the authoritative invocation list, including refused invocations. The immutable client attempts exactly one daemon request per process execution before printing a response or returning for a refusal. Each request carries the actual process `argv` and `refusal` (null, or a nonempty reason for an unknown command, wrong arity or unreadable FILE); a refusal cannot dispatch a command. The daemon stores the request and exact response hash before releasing the response. The transcript check does not infer arguments, FILE contents or refusals from shell text or output. It checks a complete, valid-JSON transcript with unique tool-use and tool-result ids and no results or permission denials referring to unknown uses. Every non-denied Bash tool call must have a result and be exactly one literal `./thesis-agent` execution under the grammar below. Other shell forms, scripts, loops, expansions, redirections and direct-socket commands fail closed, as does a tool outside Read, Write and Bash. Permission-denied Bash calls count as zero executions. For an attributable session the number of literal executions must equal the number of daemon call records, and every record must carry a string-list `argv` and null or nonempty-string `refusal`. Refusals are counted from those records, never excused from the execution count. An unrecorded execution, extra record, invalid envelope, missing result or anything unattributable fails the session's check closed. A transport or recording failure is not a local-refusal exemption. No individual transcript-to-record argument mapping is claimed.

For (b), the complete Bash command must match this grammar (SP/HT are space/tab; no CR, LF or NUL is allowed):

```text
command := (SP|HT)* "./thesis-agent" ((SP|HT)+ literal)* (SP|HT)*
literal := [a-zA-Z0-9_./:@%+,=-]+
         | single-quoted text containing no single quote
         | double-quoted text containing no double quote, $, backtick or backslash
```

Quoted text may be empty. Quoted and unquoted fragments cannot be concatenated into one argument. Literal quoted shell metacharacters are data, not shell programs. Arity and FILE readability are not part of this grammar; their refusals must also reach the daemon.
