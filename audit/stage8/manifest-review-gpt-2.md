## Blocking findings

### 1. B1: the exposure ledger is unfinished, and relabelling evidence cannot repair every prior choice

The revision correctly changes the boundary from stage labels to information exposure and prohibits calling exposed evidence fresh. However:

- `exposure.md` expressly omits earlier sessions.
- Its stated scope—“derived from stage 8 recordings”—is narrower than the revision’s cross-stage boundary.
- The existing entry does not identify exact snapshot/artifact versions or the full recipient chain. Earlier exposures still need their affected series, intervals and derived information identified sufficiently to determine overlap.

Compilation must precede **any affected selection**, not merely the next class annex. That includes source, population, feature, window and transformation choices.

There is also a substantive limit to “search or spent”: those labels protect freshness accounting, but do not retrospectively satisfy requirements that universe rules and annex choices precede observation inspection. An annex must identify which choices already occurred after exposure and which claims consequently remain blocked. A fresh reviewer does not repair that ordering.

**Required:** complete and freeze the cross-stage ledger before affected selection resumes; trace indirect and cross-source overlap; distinguish legitimate search use from a missed preregistration requirement.

### 2. The continuing recording authorization exceeds the frozen exception

The 22:04 revision restores the exact integrity whitelist. **That revision does not loosen it.**

The 22:18 revision nevertheless describes recording as authorized for “the data-layer feeds, Databento’s pulls.” The frozen criteria’s pre-annex recording exception specifically concerns **the keyless sources**. Accepting keys and purchases changes the manifest’s source-selection procedure; it does not itself amend that frozen exception.

As written, “Databento’s pulls” is not confined to documentation or ordinary catalog metadata: the source proposal also includes statistics and optional price observations.

**Required:** limit pre-annex recording to the existing exception and permitted documentation/catalog access. Additional market-observation ingestion needs the applicable annex first, or an explicit, reviewed criteria amendment before the affected access. The supplied text does not establish that prohibited price ingestion has already happened.

### 3. B3–B5: admission gates do not fully govern earlier processing and AI disclosures

The operation-specific permissions requirement is a substantial improvement. It correctly says that a role label or quotation of a prohibition supplies no permission.

The remaining problem is **when that requirement applies**:

- The permissions record is required for **admission**, while recording remains authorized now.
- Databento certification is asserted to cover acquisition, storage and deterministic kernel processing, but the supporting determination is not supplied here.
- AI-facing outputs remain a human determination.
- The AI debugging allowance “stands as written.” Its small-sample discretion is restricted before annex freeze, but is not itself source-specific contractual permission afterward.

**The two terms questions can correctly remain with the human.** Their unresolved operations must remain blocked, however. Human ownership of a decision does not authorize the operation while the answer is pending.

**Required:** apply the relevant permission gate before acquisition, processing, disclosure or retention occurs—not only before research admission. Until the human resolves the applicable AI-disclosure question, debugging samples and reports cannot use that unresolved route. This is an authorization gap, **not proof of a terms violation**.

Also preserve C8.6’s stronger restriction: an otherwise whitelisted integrity check must not disclose a holdout-derived diagnostic to the research process.
