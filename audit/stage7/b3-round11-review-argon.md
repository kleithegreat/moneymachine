### Findings (Ranked by Severity)

#### 1. [Medium] `rules-revision.md` restates resolution *"so that it reads without the text above"*, but omits two `Stored.validAt` dataset-invalidity rules (out-of-range eligible `fetchedAt` and one entity mapped to different coins) and leaves `"in range"` vs. `"§6's range"` unanchored between `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]` and `[−2^63, 2^63 − 1]`

- **Exact place:** [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L18), [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L24-L37), [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L44), [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L68-L76), [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L96-L109), and [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L146-L152); compared against [`Stored.validAt`](reference/lean/Ref/Resolve.lean#L57-L61) and [`timeline`](reference/lean/Ref/Resolve.lean#L127-L130) in [Resolve.lean](reference/lean/Ref/Resolve.lean), [`resolveCase`](reference/lean/Main.lean#L505-L514) in [Main.lean](reference/lean/Main.lean), [RULES.md](kernel/RULES.md#L182-L183) and [RULES.md](kernel/RULES.md#L197), [`HAND`](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L206-L207) / [`HAND`](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L217-L222) / [`HAND`](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L327) in [resolver_suite.py](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py), and [c76-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/c76-revision.md#L114).
- **The defect:**
  1. **Omitted `Stored.validAt` conditions:** [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L18) tells clean-room implementers (including GPT's resolver, written from `RULES.md`, `SOURCES.md`, and §7 alone without seeing the suite) that this text *"restates resolution whole, so that it reads without the text above; where that text differs, this governs."* However, [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L28-L37) preserves only one of [`Stored.validAt`](reference/lean/Ref/Resolve.lean#L57-L61)'s three validity clauses (*"as when two eligible revisions of one key share a fetchedAt"*), dropping:
     - **Eligible `fetchedAt` range (`el.all (inRange ·.fetchedAt)`):** [RULES.md](kernel/RULES.md#L182-L183)'s rule that an eligible revision (`fetchedAt ≤ V`) with `fetchedAt` outside `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]` makes the dataset invalid is nowhere in `rules-revision.md` (§7 line 96 says `"fetchedAt": INT`, and `SOURCES.md` §2 line 146 says `fetchedAt, a non-null Int64`). That precondition is also what keeps a retraction's `max(r.fetchedAt, prev)` ([rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L75), [`timeline`](reference/lean/Ref/Resolve.lean#L127-L130)) inside `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]` without a separate overflow check.
     - **Entity-to-coin consistency (`(cs.map (·.1)).eraseDups.length == cs.length`):** [RULES.md](kernel/RULES.md#L197)'s B2 rule that eligible revisions of one entity mapping to different coins make the dataset invalid is omitted from the restated §6, even though [c76-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/c76-revision.md#L114) requires Named Test 5 to test it.
  2. **Two unnamed ranges:** Within [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L24-L111), the only explicit interval is `−2^63 to 2^63 − 1` (lines [26](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L26) and [44](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L44), called *"the one range both representations carry exactly"*, and called *"§6's range"* in §7 at line [109](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L109)), while lines [32](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L32), [35](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L35), and [69](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L69) write bare *"in range"* / *"out of range"* when they mean [RULES.md](kernel/RULES.md#L38) §2's `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]`.
- **Concrete scenario:**
  1. **`HAND` case 32** ([resolver_suite.py](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L206), [resolver_hand.jsonl](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_hand.jsonl#L33)): `bars` at `V = 100` with `rev("bars", 100, b(1))` and `rev("bars", -2**62 - 1, b(2))`. Because `−2^62 − 1` fits in `Int64`, [`image`](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L613-L621) is `True`, so all four C7.6 participants evaluate it (and `HAND` case 33 at line 207 tests `−2^63 − 1` on GPT and Lean). In `fetchedAt` order, `−2^62 − 1` is the key's *first* eligible revision: *last* does not exist, so step 2 does not raise `knownAt` to `fetchedAt`, and its rule `eventTime + 120000` yields `36120000` (in range); the second revision at `fetchedAt = 100` raises to `36120000` (in range). An implementation following [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md) *"without the text above"* resolves `HAND` case 32 to two timeline entries, while Lean (`refmm resolve`) and `HAND` require `"INVALID"`.
  2. **`HAND` cases 46 & 115 vs. 52 & 53** ([resolver_suite.py](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L217-L222), [L327](audit/stage7/b3-draft/resolver_suite.py#L327)): reading bare *"in range"* at lines 32 and 35 as `−2^63 to 2^63 − 1` resolves `eventTime = 2^62` (case 46) and lineage `e = 2^62` (case 115) instead of returning `"INVALID"`, whereas reading *"§6's range"* at line 109 as `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]` rejects `close = 2^63 − 1` and `−2^63` (cases 52–53) as `"INVALID"` instead of resolving.
- **Outcome it could change:** C7.6's 4-way exact-agreement verdict (GPT's clean-room resolver or slice-5 loaders failing against Lean and `HAND` on cases 32, 33, 46, 52, 53, 115) and C7.6 Named Test 5.
- **Design change or wording fix:** **Wording fix only.** In [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L28-L37), state all dataset-invalidity rules directly (two eligible revisions of one key sharing a `fetchedAt`, an eligible revision's `fetchedAt` outside §2's range `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]`, eligible revisions of one entity mapping to different coins, or any eligible revision failing to decode; revisions with `fetchedAt > V` are not eligible and not examined), name `§2's range [−2^62, 2^62 − 1]` explicitly at lines 32, 35, and 68–69, and write `−2^63 to 2^63 − 1` instead of `§6's range` at line 109.

#### 2. [Low] `SOURCES.md` §2 (`rules-revision.md`) leaves file-stem irrelevance and non-string `upstream` column-type scope implicit

- **Exact place:** [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L142-L154) (`SOURCES.md` §2), compared against [`Table::read`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L196-L202) in [market.rs](kernel/mm/src/market.rs), [`Store._files`](ingest/store.py#L64-L66) and [`Store.write`](ingest/store.py#L103) in [store.py](ingest/store.py), [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L25-L37), and [c76-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/c76-revision.md#L56-L60).
- **The defect:**
  1. **Parquet file discovery vs. filename stems:** [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L142-L143) says *"the store holds a dataset's revisions as Parquet files under its directory, each row one revision"*, without stating explicitly that every `.parquet` file in the directory belongs to the dataset regardless of its filename stem (unlike `mm`'s current [`Table::read`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L199-L201), which requires an `i64` stem and filters `stem <= version`, whereas [`Store._files`](ingest/store.py#L66) reads every `*.parquet`). Because C7.6's Parquet image (`0.parquet`, `1.parquet`, …) and Test 3's [`staged`](ingest/tests/test_store.py#L235) (`0.parquet`, `1.parquet` at `V = 100`) use small integer stems `<= V` on almost every case, stem-filtering assumptions are easy to carry forward into slice 5 unless ruled out in the text.
  2. **`upstream` column type on tombstones and post-$V$ rows:** [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L152-L154) states *"A derived source's `upstream`, a string column, holds its lineage as a JSON text read by RULES.md §7's mapping; a null, or a file without the column, is a lineage that does not decode, and a tombstone's is never read."* Unlike footer checks (line [147](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L147)) and declared columns (lines [154-157](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L154-L157)), this leaves ambiguous whether an `upstream` column of non-string Arrow type (e.g., `Null` from an untyped all-null write, or `Int64`) fails at the file level or only when decoding an eligible non-tombstone derived row.
- **Concrete scenario:**
  1. On a store written by [`Store.write`](ingest/store.py#L103) holding `100.parquet` and `200.parquet` (where `200.parquet` contains a holdout-crossing event time or null `fetchedAt`) queried at `V = 100`, or holding `part-0.parquet`, a loader retaining [`market.rs`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L200-L201)'s `i64`-stem or `stem <= version` filter skips `200.parquet` / `part-0.parquet`, missing holdout crossings or rows that Python's [`Store`](ingest/store.py#L66) reads (and on generated C7.6 stores with `V = 0`, drops `1.parquet` rows with `fetchedAt = 0`).
  2. On a derived source whose second file holds only a tombstone (`fetchedAt ≤ V`, `tombstone = true`) or a post-$V$ row whose `upstream` column has Arrow type `Null` or `Int64`, Loader A (checking `upstream`'s type per eligible non-tombstone row) resolves the store, whereas Loader B (requiring any present `upstream` column on a derived source to be `Utf8`/`LargeUtf8` per file) returns `"INVALID"`.
- **Outcome it could change:** Holdout-check coverage on `{fetched_at}.parquet` / non-numeric-stem store files (I8), and Python-vs-`mm` agreement on C7.6 Named Test 2 and `V = 0` stores.
- **Design change or wording fix:** **Wording fix only.** In [rules-revision.md](audit/stage7/b3-draft/rules-revision.md#L142-L154), state *"as every `.parquet` file under its directory (file names carry no timestamp or version meaning)"*, and state that for an eligible non-tombstone derived row `upstream` decodes only from a non-null string column (any other type, a null, or a file without the column is a lineage that does not decode; on tombstones and post-$V$ rows `upstream` is never read, whatever its type).
