# Independent Review — Stage 7, Slice 5 (`7983958..cb0d62d`)

Slice 5’s core bitemporal resolution engines ([`ingest/resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py), [`ingest/knownat.py`](ingest/knownat.py), [`kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs), and [`kernel/mm/src/rule.rs`](kernel/mm/src/rule.rs)) match [`RULES.md` §6–§7](kernel/RULES.md#L186-L367) and Lean’s [`Ref/Resolve.lean`](reference/lean/Ref/Resolve.lean), and neither loader decodes any non-event-time column or reads column statistics before the holdout check passes.

## 1. Findings (Ranked by Severity)

### F1 [Critical / High] — [`Market`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L40-L47)’s `NaN` representation of retractions loses `role = None`, breaking `fresh`, trading on retracted bars/funding, and bypassing I9’s `$10,000` order-notional cap
- **Where:** [`market::load`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L240-L278) (`Funding` pushed with `rate: f64::NAN, premium: f64::NAN` at L246; `Bar` pushed with `NaN` OHLCV and `trades: 0` at L265–L276), [`sim::run`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L448-L478), and [`sim::check_fill`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L212).
- **Defect:** [`RULES.md` §6](kernel/RULES.md#L276-L286) (*“A retraction … delivers no value and no role payload”*) and Lean’s [`Ref.Sim.Ctx.step`](reference/lean/Ref/Sim.lean#L389-L393) + [`Ctx.admissible`](reference/lean/Ref/Sim.lean#L446-L449) require `fresh` at `t_d = B + delta` to check `o.key == B && o.role.isSome` for both `settlement` and `bars`; if either is retracted at `t_d`, `fresh` is `false` and the simulator must emit `Hold { why: "stale" }` (which [`ingest/witness.py`](ingest/witness.py#L159-L162) implements via `seen(...) is None`). Because [`market::load`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L240-L278) pushes retractions as ordinary [`Funding`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L23-L30) and [`Bar`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L10-L21) structs with `event == B`, [`sim.rs`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L448) (`f.event == big_b` and `x.event == big_b`) evaluates **`fresh = true`** on retracted keys.
- **Concrete scenarios:**
  1. *I9 `$10,000` notional-cap bypass on a retracted bar:* Coin `0` holds `held = 200.0` (worth `$50,000` at `$250`). Key `(0, B)` in `bars_4h` has a first revision known by `t_d`, then a tombstone or rejected correction fetched by `t_d`, and bar `(0, B + 4h)` is live (`open = 250.0`, `trades > 0`). Any program whose weight at `t_d` is `0.0` (e.g. `{"const": 0.0, "unit": "1"}` or W0 inside its deadband) evaluates `Some(0.0)`: `fresh` is `true`; `price = machine.bar[0].unwrap().close` is `NaN` ([`sim.rs`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L468)); `qty = -held = -200.0` (L471); the I9 clamp `if qty.abs() * price > MAX_ORDER_NOTIONAL` (`NaN > 10_000.0`, L472) is **`false`**, leaving `qty = -200.0`; `if qty != 0.0 && (... || w == 0.0)` (L475) is **`true`**, queuing [`Order`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L41-L45) with `price: NaN`. At `B + 4h`, all `-200.0` (`$50,000` notional, **5× the `$10,000` cap**) fills, and [`check_fill`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L212)’s invariant guard (`f.qty.abs() * f.decision_price > MAX_ORDER_NOTIONAL * ...`, where `decision_price = NaN`) also evaluates to **`false`**.
  2. *Trading through retracted settlement:* `(0, B)` in `funding` is retracted by `t_d` while `bars_4h` at `(0, B)` is live. Any program not reading `funding` (e.g. `const 0.05` or bar momentum) sees `fresh = true` and places live orders instead of holding `"stale"`.
  3. *Dropped trace event / wrong hold reason:* When `bars_4h` is retracted (`price = NaN`) and `w != 0.0`, `qty` is `NaN`, so neither `Hold` nor `Decision` is emitted for `(t_d, coin)`; when `w` reads the retracted input (`None`), `mm` emits `Hold { why: "undefined" }` instead of Lean’s `Hold { why: "stale" }`.
- **Outcome changed:** Breaks [`INVARIANTS.md` I9](kernel/INVARIANTS.md#L17) (`$10,000` per-order notional cap) silently, trades on retracted intervals instead of holding, alters simulated returns/Sharpe/promotion decisions, and breaks differential agreement with `refmm` and [`witness.py`](ingest/witness.py).
- **Fix:** Carry an explicit `pub retraction: bool` (or `Option` role payload) on [`market::Funding`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L23-L30) and [`market::Bar`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L10-L21) (and in [`mmdump`](reference/mmdump/src/main.rs)), and require `!f.retraction` and `!x.retraction` in [`sim::run`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L448).

### F2 [High] — Event-source delivery order breaks `(recording time, eventTime)` tie-breaking
- **Where:** [`market::keys`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L216-L220), [`market::load`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L282-L286), and [`sim::run`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L263-L265) (plus [`mmdump`](reference/mmdump/src/main.rs#L67-L68)).
- **Defect:** [`SOURCES.md` §4](kernel/SOURCES.md#L133) and [`Ref/Sim.lean`](reference/lean/Ref/Sim.lean#L224-L225) specify that observations of an event source sharing `(knownAt, coin)` are delivered in **`(recording time, eventTime)`** (`(fetchedAt, key)`) order, and only the last delivered observation remains in `MState.latest` (supplying a value iff `slot == hour(t)`). Slice 5 sorted [`market::keys`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L219) by `(k.coin, k.event)` and dropped `e.fetched`, while [`sim.rs`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L264) still sorts `e_order` by `(e.known, e.coin, *slot, i)`—breaking ties by **`(eventTime, fetchedAt)`** instead of **`(fetchedAt, eventTime)`**.
- **Concrete scenario:** In `events_forum` (`rule = eventTime + delta`), coin `0` has:
  - Key `10 * HOUR`: first revision at `fetchedAt = 10 * HOUR + 1_000` (`count = 2`), and a correction at `fetchedAt = 15 * HOUR + delta` (`count = 3`, so `knownAt = 15 * HOUR + delta`, `recorded = 15 * HOUR + delta`, `slot = 10 * HOUR`).
  - Key `15 * HOUR`: first revision at `fetchedAt = 15 * HOUR + 1_000` (`count = 5`, so `knownAt = 15 * HOUR + delta`, `recorded = 15 * HOUR + 1_000`, `slot = 15 * HOUR`).
  At `tick = 15 * HOUR + delta`, Lean delivers key `15h` (`recorded = 15h + 1_000`) *before* key `10h`’s correction (`recorded = 15h + delta`), so the correction overwrites `latest` with `slot = 10h != 15h` and `forum_posts` evaluates to **`undefined` (`None`)**. `mm` places key `10h` before key `15h` in `market.events`, delivering `15h` last and evaluating `forum_posts` to **`Some(5.0)`**.
- **Outcome changed:** Changes event-feed DSL inputs between `undefined` and finite values, altering strategy weights, trades, returns, promotion verdicts, and `refmm` agreement.
- **Fix:** Retain `recorded: i64` (`e.fetched`) and `key_event: i64` (`k.event`) on [`market::Event`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L32-L38) and sort `e_order` in [`sim::run`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L264) by `(e.known, e.coin, *slot, e.recorded, e.key_event)`.

### F3 [High] — Post-`V` files corrupt or crash [`Store.as_of`](ingest/store.py#L188-L191) / [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181-L186), and both loaders decode post-`V` column chunks before filtering `fetchedAt <= V`
- **Where:** [`Store.source`](ingest/store.py#L145-L155) (L151), [`Store._frame`](ingest/store.py#L166-L180) (L170–L178), [`ingest/resolve.py::read_revisions`](ingest/resolve.py#L188-L194), and [`resolve.rs::read_revisions`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L458-L469).
- **Defect:** [`RULES.md` §6](kernel/RULES.md#L221) (*“revisions fetched after V are not examined”*) and [`SOURCES.md` §2](kernel/SOURCES.md#L120-L121) (*“a file whose rows are all after V, may hold any Parquet type and any values without making the dataset invalid”*) forbid post-`V`-only files from affecting resolution at `V`.
  1. [`Store._frame`](ingest/store.py#L170-L178) seeds `parts` with empty Arrow tables typed from `f.types[c]` for **every** `f in footers` (including post-`V`-only files) and runs `pl.concat(parts, how="diagonal_relaxed")`, while [`Store.source`](ingest/store.py#L151) includes columns that exist only in post-`V` files.
  2. Both [`read_revisions`](ingest/resolve.py#L188) implementations project and Arrow-decode all `wanted` columns (`tombstone`, `declared`, `upstream`) **before** checking whether any row in the file has `fetchedAt <= version`.
- **Concrete scenarios:**
  1. `0.parquet` (`fetchedAt = 50`) has `coin="BTC", eventTime=E, rate=1.5` (`Float64`). Later, `1.parquet` (`fetchedAt = 200`) stores `rate` as `List(Float64)` (the exact post-`V` fixture of [`test_parquet_cells.py`](kernel/tests/rules/test_parquet_cells.py#L64)) or `Utf8` (`"1.5"`). Calling `store.as_of("bars_4h", K, version=100)` raises `polars.exceptions.SchemaError` on `List(Float64)` and **silently coerces `rate` from `Float64` `[1.5]` to `String` `["1.5"]`** on `Utf8`!
  2. `1.parquet` (`fetchedAt = 200`) has valid footer metadata and `fetchedAt` column, but invalid UTF-8 bytes in `close` or `upstream`. At `version = 100`, [`resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L460) returns `Refusal::Invalid` and [`resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py#L188) raises uncaught `ArrowInvalid` instead of ignoring the post-`V` file.
- **Outcome changed:** Appending post-`V` files changes or crashes version-pinned [`Store.as_of`](ingest/store.py#L188) / [`witness.py`](ingest/witness.py) outputs and can turn a valid dataset at `V` into `INVALID` in `mm`. (Note also that [`Store.write`](ingest/store.py#L136) casts `pl.Null` unconditionally to `pl.Float64`; if an earlier file had `pl.Boolean` in that column, `diagonal_relaxed` in `_frame` raises `SchemaError`, whereas casting to `old.schema.get(c, pl.Float64)` preserves the prior column type.)
- **Fix:** In both `read_revisions`, project `["fetchedAt"]` first, verify `fetchedAt` has no nulls, compute `eligible` (`fetchedAt <= version`), and decode the remaining `wanted` columns only if `eligible` is non-empty. In [`Store._frame`](ingest/store.py#L166-L180), build the frame from `tables` alone when `tables` is non-empty, falling back to cell-typed columns only when `tables` is empty.

### F4 [Medium-High] — Multi-dataset `holdout_check` short-circuits per source, violating uncheckable-precedence and least-crossing-time across datasets
- **Where:** [`resolve::resolve`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L517-L521) and [`resolve.resolve`](ingest/resolve.py#L232-L234).
- **Defect:** Both resolvers iterate `for (s, fs) in sources.zip(footers): holdout_check(fs, s.event, boundary)?`. Because [`holdout_check`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L330-L333) returns `Err` at the end of a single source when `least` is `Some`, a crossing in an earlier source aborts before later sources are checked. C7.6 Named Test 3 ([`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1143-L1161)) requires across the call’s datasets (`market::load` always resolves `["funding", "bars_4h", ...events]` together) that (1) *“Any uncheckable event time takes precedence over crossings, regardless of file order”*, and (2) a crossing refusal reports *“the least crossing event time”*. (No non-event-time column is decoded because `Err` still returns before `read_revisions`.)
- **Concrete scenario:** `funding/0.parquet` has `eventTime = holdout_start + 2 * HOUR`, while `bars_4h/0.parquet` has either an uncheckable `eventTime` (`null`, `Float64`, or out-of-range) or an earlier crossing `holdout_start + HOUR`. `market::load` checks `funding` first and reports `holdout_start + 2 * HOUR` instead of `UNCHECKABLE` or `holdout_start + HOUR`.
- **Outcome changed:** Emits the wrong I8 / C7.6 holdout refusal diagnostic in `mm eval` and multi-source `resolve` calls.
- **Fix:** Pass all `(source, footers)` pairs into `holdout_check` together so `UNCHECKABLE` in any source wins immediately and `least` is minimized across all sources before returning the crossing refusal.

### F5 [Medium] — [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181-L186) delivers a rejected revision’s stored values on retractions, and C7.6 Named Test 1 (`Observations`) bypasses both production observation surfaces and lacks its positive controls
- **Where:** [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181-L186), [`Store._frame`](ingest/store.py#L166-L180), [`loaders.py`](kernel/tests/rules/loaders.py#L74-L109), [`test_suite.py`](kernel/tests/rules/test_suite.py#L103-L112), and [`kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs#L205-L218).
- **Defect:**
  1. [`RULES.md` §6](kernel/RULES.md#L276-L279) (*“A retraction … delivers no value”*) and C7.6 Named Test 1 ([`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1126-L1130)) forbid delivering stored values on a retraction—explicitly listing *“a retraction’s stored values”* as one of Test 1’s three required positive controls. While [`Entry`](ingest/resolve.py#L284-L285) sets `e.cells = None`, [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181-L186) ignores `e.cells` and indexes `tables[t].take([r])` via `e.where`, returning the **raw stored cells** of a rejected live revision alongside `retraction = True`. ([`witness.py`](ingest/witness.py#L91) guards on `retraction` before reading `value`, avoiding corruption there, but [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181) itself violates the rule.)
  2. [`test_observations_match_lean`](kernel/tests/rules/test_suite.py#L103-L111) missed both [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181) and [`market::load`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L223) (Findings F1 & F2) because [`loaders.python`](kernel/tests/rules/loaders.py#L74-L101) synthesizes `"observations"` inside the test harness (`delivery` at L87) instead of calling `Store.entries`, [`loaders.rust`](kernel/tests/rules/loaders.py#L104-L109) calls `mm resolve` (`Resolved::observations`) instead of `market::load`, and **none** of Named Test 1’s three required positive controls ([`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1129-L1130)) or Named Test 2/4’s controls ([`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1125)) exist. In addition, Named Test 3’s Rust namesake ([`kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs#L205-L218)) omits the `evidence` late-rows variant required by [`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1186-L1187).
- **Concrete scenario:** Write `funding` row `(coin="BTC", eventTime=2*H, reportedTime=2*H+50, fundingRate=0.01)` at `fetchedAt=100`, then write `(coin="BTC", eventTime=2*H, reportedTime=None, fundingRate=0.99)` at `fetchedAt=200`. `store.entries("funding", 200)` returns row 1 with `retraction=True` and **`fundingRate=0.99`** instead of `None`.
- **Outcome changed:** Exposes rejected revisions’ values on [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181) and leaves C7.6 Named Test 1 blind to production observation-layer bugs (such as F1 and F2).
- **Fix:** Mask value columns to `null` where `retraction` is `True` in [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181-L186); test the actual production observation surfaces in Named Test 1 and add its three positive controls; and add the `evidence` late-rows fixture to [`kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs#L205-L218).

### F6 [Medium] — Uncaught Arrow/IO exceptions in Python `holdout_check` / `read_revisions`, and decoding `event` pages before checking `Footer`’s column type in both loaders
- **Where:** [`ingest/resolve.py::holdout_check`](ingest/resolve.py#L124-L126), [`ingest/resolve.py::read_revisions`](ingest/resolve.py#L188), and [`resolve.rs::holdout_check`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L313-L319).
- **Defect:**
  1. [`Footer.__init__`](ingest/resolve.py#L94-L95) catches `(pa.ArrowException, OSError, ValueError)` and raises `Refusal("INVALID")`, **but [`holdout_check`](ingest/resolve.py#L124) and [`read_revisions`](ingest/resolve.py#L188) do not**. A file with an intact footer and corrupted column-chunk bytes in the body raises raw `pyarrow.lib.ArrowInvalid` / `OSError` in `read_revisions`, violating [`RULES.md` §7](kernel/RULES.md#L334) (*“Host exceptions … are never language results”*) and diverging from Rust (`batch.map_err(invalid)?` at [`resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L460)).
  2. Both `holdout_check` implementations already have `Footer`’s schema (`f.types` / `f.type_of(event)`), yet **decode `event`’s data pages before checking that its type is `Int64`**. If `eventTime` has a non-`Int64` type whose page fails Arrow decoding (e.g. `Utf8` with invalid UTF-8 bytes), Python raises `pyarrow.lib.ArrowInvalid`—a **subclass of `ValueError`**, violating C7.6’s *“raises exactly `ValueError`, not a subclass, with the applicable message”* ([`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1150))—and Rust hits `batch.map_err(invalid)?` (`Refusal::Invalid` → `LoadError::Io`, exit 3, unrecorded in ledger) instead of `Refusal::Holdout(UNCHECKABLE)` (`R_HOLDOUT`, exit 1, recorded abort in ledger).
- **Scenario & outcome changed:** A search store file with `eventTime: Utf8` containing invalid UTF-8 bytes causes `mm eval` to exit 3 (`IO`, uncounted against the agent’s budget/ledger) instead of exit 1 (`R_HOLDOUT`, counted abort), and raises `ArrowInvalid` instead of exact `ValueError(UNCHECKABLE)` in Python; a body-corrupted file in unbounded/holdout mode crashes Python callers instead of raising `Refusal("INVALID")`.
- **Fix:** Check `f.types.get(event) != pa.int64()` / `f.type_of(event) != Some(&DataType::Int64)` in `holdout_check` **before** opening/decoding `event`’s column chunks; and wrap `pq.read_table` in `ingest/resolve.py` in `except (pa.ArrowException, OSError)` raising `Refusal("INVALID", ...)`.

### F7 [Medium] — `Footer` in both loaders validates `ARROW:schema` subtrees of undeclared columns
- **Where:** [`Footer.__init__`](ingest/resolve.py#L90-L95) and [`Footer::read`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L247).
- **Defect:** [`SOURCES.md` §2](kernel/SOURCES.md#L113) (and L122) states: *“Nested leaves are not top-level fields (a List column is one field); **undeclared columns’ subtrees are ignored**.”* Both `file.schema_arrow` in PyArrow and `ArrowReaderMetadata::load(..., ArrowReaderOptions::new())` in `parquet` reconcile `ARROW:schema` against the Parquet schema across **all** columns, returning `INVALID` if an undeclared column’s `ARROW:schema` subtree conflicts with its Parquet physical/logical subtree.
- **Concrete scenario & outcome changed:** A `bars_4h` Parquet file has identical ordered top-level names in both schemas including an undeclared column `extra` that is physical `INT64` in Parquet but `Struct<x: Int64>` in `ARROW:schema`. Both loaders reject the dataset as `INVALID` instead of resolving `bars_4h`.
- **Fix:** Load Parquet metadata with `ArrowReaderOptions::new().with_skip_arrow_metadata(true)` (Rust) / Parquet root schema descriptors (Python), compare top-level names against the decoded `ARROW:schema` IPC buffer, and resolve Arrow types only for `fetchedAt`, `tombstone`, and `columns_read`.

### F8 [Medium] — Two-run check gaps: `late_venue_state` leaves `bars_4h`/`premium` decision inputs untested and compares execution-gated trace events; the daemon holdout check has no read-ahead mutant control
- **Where:** [`noninterference.rs`](kernel/mm/tests/noninterference.rs#L20-L53) & L88–L104, [`test_agent_daemon.py`](gate/tests/test_agent_daemon.py#L142-L172), and [`mutants.py`](kernel/mutants/mutants.py#L59-L68).
- **Defect:**
  1. [`a_late_venue_state_revision_leaves_every_decision_input_alone`](kernel/mm/tests/noninterference.rs#L88-L104) writes late corrections to both `funding` and `bars_4h` (`close * 1.2` at L95), yet [`PROGRAM`](kernel/mm/tests/noninterference.rs#L20-L27) **reads only `funding` and `forum_posts`**—never `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, or `premium`. Moreover, [`decisions(r, false)`](kernel/mm/tests/noninterference.rs#L44-L53) compares `Event::Decision` trace entries, which [`sim::run`](kernel/mm/src/sim.rs#L469-L477) suppresses whenever `qty == 0.0` or `|qty| * price < min_notional`—conditions that depend on execution state (`e1`, `held`), which the late venue-state revision *does* alter!
  2. [`rust-port-plan.md`](audit/stage7/rust-port-plan.md#L72-L74) requires that each of the three two-run checks *“gets a positive control: **a mutant that reads ahead must fail it**.”* Unlike `S1` and `S2` in [`mutants.py`](kernel/mutants/mutants.py#L59-L68), [`test_replies_do_not_depend_on_the_holdouts_bytes`](gate/tests/test_agent_daemon.py#L151-L172) has **no read-ahead mutant** in `mutants.py` or in the test; L172 only scales `markets["search"]` on the unmutated daemon, proving replies depend on `store/` rather than proving a read-ahead daemon/kernel mutant is caught.
- **Concrete scenario & outcome changed:** (a) A mutant feeding final `bar_at` (`close * 1.2`) into `Machine::on_bar` before `knownAt` passes `late_venue_state` undetected; conversely, a strategy whose `|w * e1|` crosses `min_notional` (`$10`) when execution equity `e1` moves fails `decisions(clean, false) == decisions(late, false)` despite identical decision inputs. (b) `mutants.py` never exercises `test_agent_daemon.py` against a holdout-reading mutant.
- **Fix:** Make `PROGRAM` in [`noninterference.rs`](kernel/mm/tests/noninterference.rs#L20-L27) read all venue-state inputs (`funding`, `premium`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, plus `forum_posts`) and compare decision inputs / raw weights prior to `e1`/`held`/`min_notional` order gating; and add a `D1` mutant (e.g. `Daemon` passing `holdout` alongside `store` or disclosing a holdout evaluation) that must fail `assert replies(markets["holdout"]) == clean`.

### F9 [Low-Medium] — Path reopening and `(size, mtime_ns)` cache identity instead of pinning file bytes
- **Where:** [`Store._pinned`](ingest/store.py#L91-L100), [`ingest/resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py#L124) & L188, [`Footer::read` / `Footer::batches`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L247-L288), and [`market::load`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L237).
- **Defect:** [`SOURCES.md` §7](kernel/SOURCES.md#L215-L216) (*“Evaluation reads those admitted bytes, never a reopened path”*) and C7.6 ([`audit/criteria.md`](audit/criteria.md#L1182-L1185): *“Whatever is decoded after the check comes only from those bytes”*) forbid reopening paths after admission. Python keys `self._cache[dataset]` by `(f, st.st_size, st.st_mtime_ns)` and reopens `f.path` across `Footer`, `holdout_check`, `read_revisions`, and `revisions`; Rust reopens each `.parquet` path four times (`Footer::read`, `holdout_check`, `read_revisions`, and `fs::read` for `data_hash`).
- **Concrete scenario & outcome changed:** An equal-size in-place overwrite of a `.parquet` file (e.g. changing an 8-byte uncompressed `Int64` `eventTime` to `>= HOLDOUT_START`) within coarse `mtime_ns` resolution or with preserved mtime leaves `identity` unchanged and `kept["checked"] == True` in [`Store._pinned`](ingest/store.py#L92-L99), allowing a subsequent `store.as_of(dataset, t, new_version)` call to skip `holdout_check` and decode the holdout row; in Rust, a concurrent file swap between `holdout_check` and `read_revisions` decodes un-admitted bytes.
- **Fix:** Read each file into memory once (`bytes::Bytes` in Rust, `pa.py_buffer(path.read_bytes())` in Python), key Python’s cache by `(f, sha256(buf))`, and run footer checks, `holdout_check`, `read_revisions`, and `data_hash` on those exact buffers.

## 2. Structural Review of the Holdout Decode Limit (Prompt Check 2 / C7.6)

### Plain Answer
**Before `holdout_check` succeeds across a call, NO column other than the declared event-time column is decoded, cached, or used, NO Parquet footer column statistics of any column are read or used, and NO result-bearing output (timelines, DataFrames, `data_hash`, simulation trace, or metrics) is produced on any success, refusal, diagnostic, or cleanup path in either loader.**

The only defects on the holdout path are **F4** (multi-source short-circuiting changes which holdout refusal message is reported when multiple datasets fail at once), **F6** (`event` pages are decoded before checking `Footer`’s `event` type is `Int64`), and **F9** (file paths are reopened rather than read from pinned bytes).

### Path-by-Path Trace

1. **Python ([`Store`](ingest/store.py#L72-L192) → [`ingest/resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py)):**
   - **Entry points:** [`Store.as_of`](ingest/store.py#L188), [`Store.entries`](ingest/store.py#L181), [`Store.revisions`](ingest/store.py#L102), and [`Store.write`](ingest/store.py#L109) (via `self.revisions`) all call [`Store._pinned`](ingest/store.py#L87-L100) first; direct callers of [`resolve.resolve`](ingest/resolve.py#L224-L235) run `check_declarations`, then `read_footers`, then `holdout_check` before `read_revisions`.
   - **Step 1 — Directory & Footer (`Footer.__init__`, [`resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py#L84-L103)):** Opens `pq.ParquetFile(path)` (reads magic + footer metadata tail only; zero row-group column chunks or dictionary/data pages). Reads `num_rows`, root schema names/types, and `ARROW:schema` IPC header; checks unique ordered top-level names, `fetchedAt == Int64`, and optional `tombstone == Boolean`. No column `Statistics` (`min`/`max`/`null_count`) in the footer are accessed.
     - *Refusal/cleanup on footer failure:* Raises `Refusal("INVALID", f"{path}: {e}")` containing only path + schema/footer error. In `_pinned`, the list comprehension `[resolve.Footer(f) for f in files]` aborts *before* assigning `self._cache[dataset]`, leaving `_cache` untouched.
   - **Step 2 — Holdout Check (`holdout_check`, [`resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py#L117-L135)):** When `self.event_before is not None` (search store) and `not kept["checked"]`, `_pinned` calls `holdout_check(kept["footers"], "eventTime", self.event_before)`. Empty files (`f.rows == 0`) are skipped without page reads; non-empty files with `event in f.names` run `pq.read_table(f.path, columns=[event])`, projecting **only** the single `event` column chunk. (`pc.min_max(col)` and `pc.filter(col, ...)` execute over the decoded Arrow `ChunkedArray` of `event`, never Parquet footer statistics.)
     - *Refusal/cleanup on holdout failure:* Raises `ValueError(UNCHECKABLE)` or `ValueError(f"holdout crossing: event time {least} at or after {boundary}")`. `kept["checked"]` remains `False`, `kept["revisions"]` remains `None`, `kept["resolved"]` remains `{}`, and no caller (`as_of`, `entries`, `revisions`, `witness.run`) catches or converts it into data output.
   - **Step 3 — Post-check decode:** Only after `holdout_check` returns `None` does `_pinned` set `kept["checked"] = True` and return to `revisions` (`pl.read_parquet`) or `_resolved` (`resolve.resolve` → `read_revisions`).

2. **Rust ([`main.rs`](kernel/mm/src/main.rs) → [`market::load`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L223-L307) / `mm resolve` → [`resolve::resolve`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L504-L535)):**
   - **Entry points:** `mm eval` (`cmd_eval`, [`main.rs`](kernel/mm/src/main.rs#L284-L315)) passes `Some(policy.holdout_start)` to `market::load`; `mm holdout` (`cmd_holdout`, [`main.rs`](kernel/mm/src/main.rs#L420-L433)) passes `None` after admission gates pass; `mm resolve` (`cmd_resolve`, [`main.rs`](kernel/mm/src/main.rs#L254-L281)) passes `given.holdout`.
   - **Step 1 — Declarations & Footers ([`resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L505-L516)):** Checks `METADATA` collision (`E_DECL`) and `rule::check` (`E_RULE`) before touching disk. Then [`Footer::read`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L245-L270) calls `ArrowReaderMetadata::load` (footer tail + `ARROW:schema` IPC header only; zero data pages or column statistics read). On error, returns `Err(Refusal::Invalid)` → `LoadError::Io` → `cmd_eval` prints `{"ok":false,"code":"IO",...}` and exits `3` with nothing written to the ledger or stdout trace.
   - **Step 2 — Holdout Check ([`holdout_check`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L309-L334)):** For `f.rows > 0`, calls `f.batches(&[event])`, which constructs `ProjectionMask::roots(schema, [event_idx])` ([`resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L283))—the `parquet` crate seeks and decodes **only** `event`’s column chunks. Every value in every batch is checked directly in memory (`Int64Array`, `null_count() == 0`, `LO..=HI`, `t >= boundary`); no row-group/page `Statistics` are read.
     - *Refusal/diagnostic/cleanup on holdout failure:* Returns `Err(Refusal::Holdout(msg))` → `LoadError::Holdout(msg)` in `market::load` **before** `read_revisions` (L525) or `data_hash` (`fs::read`, [`market.rs`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L233-L239)) or `sim::run` ever execute. In [`cmd_eval`](kernel/mm/src/main.rs#L294-L303), `LoadError::Holdout(e)` records an `abort` disclosure (`{"code":"R_HOLDOUT","reason":e}`, containing only `{least}` and `{boundary}` or `UNCHECKABLE`), prints `reject_err("R_HOLDOUT", &e)`, and exits `1`.
   - **Step 3 — Post-check decode:** Only after `holdout_check` succeeds on every source do `read_revisions`, `build`, `data_hash`, and `Market` construction run.

## 6. What Was Checked and Found Sound

- **Rule grammar, static checking, and arithmetic ([`ingest/knownat.py`](ingest/knownat.py), [`kernel/mm/src/rule.rs`](kernel/mm/src/rule.rs)):** Strict RFC 8259 JSON parsing rejecting BOM, duplicate object keys, lone surrogates, non-decimal/leading-zero numbers, and NaN/Infinity; `rule == 1`, exact two-member top object, `E_DECL` preceding `E_RULE`, positive literal `floor` modulus, Euclidean `floor` (`//` in Python, `div_euclid` in Rust), non-empty `max`, and exact `[−2^62, 2^62 − 1]` range checks on literals, time fields, `add`, `floor`, `dep[d]`, and `dep[d] + lag`.
- **Core bitemporal timeline resolution ([`ingest/resolve.py`](ingest/resolve.py#L241-L326), [`kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/src/resolve.rs#L537-L705)):**
  - Cell identity (`Int(1) != Real(1.0)`, `Bool(true) != Int(1)`, `-0.0 == +0.0` canonicalized to `+0.0`, NaN/Inf rejected in declared columns on eligible rows).
  - Duplicate `(key, fetchedAt)` within or across files refused as `INVALID`; distinct `fetchedAt` revisions sorted causally regardless of file order.
  - Adjacent deduplication over `tombstone` and `declared(s)` cell identities against the immediately preceding eligible revision (`last`, even when rejected), so duplicate rejected revisions do not emit duplicate retractions and restoring an earlier value after an intervening revision is treated as a correction.
  - Effective `knownAt` monotonicity (`max(rule, fetchedAt if last is not None, entries[-1].known, *(dep[d] + lag))`), rejected first revisions emitting nothing, and rejected/tombstone revisions after a delivery emitting a retraction at `max(fetchedAt, prev.known)` preserving `prev.slot`.
  - Causal upstream derivation bound (`upstream_time`): topological DFS over `deps`, strict lineage JSON validation, requiring at least one delivered entry (including retractions) with `e.fetched <= r.fetched` on every listed upstream key.
- **Execution from final revisions (`bar_at` / `rate_at` in [`market.rs`](kernel/mm/src/market.rs#L248-L280) and `final` in [`ingest/witness.py`](ingest/witness.py#L77-L79)):** Both take each key’s final delivered entry iff `!last.retraction`, omitting retracted keys from marks, fills, and funding settlement, matching [`Ref/Sim.lean`](reference/lean/Ref/Sim.lean#L192-L202).
- **Holdout staging positive controls ([`ingest/tests/test_store.py`](ingest/tests/test_store.py#L467-L570), [`kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs`](kernel/mm/tests/resolve.rs#L229-L353)):** All four required C7.6 Named Test 3 positive controls (eager decode, footer-statistic trust, first-crossing-only/crossing-precedes-uncheckable, and unpinned cache reuse across file replacement) are present in both test suites and verified to fail their checks on the variants they target.
