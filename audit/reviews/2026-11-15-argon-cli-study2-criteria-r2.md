3. **Check 3 (D69, the guard's family — [criteria-notes.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/criteria-notes.md#L59-L64), [4-study-2.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/4-study-2.md#L60-L61), [arithmetic.py.txt](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/arithmetic.py.txt#L350-L358)):**
   - *Can `bar_mix` or equal weights be undefined?* No: for every coin in a span, `dual_share` returns a rational in $[0, 1]$ (`2*q > 0` when $q > 0$, and $0$ or $1$ when $q \le 0$, including when `events = 0` so $s_1 = q = 0$), and divides by $\max(1, \sum \text{shares}) \ge 1$.
   - *Construction where a guard rule still ends despite keeping $1/25$ in cash:* On the **daily** clock (`clock = 1440`) or **hourly** clock (`clock = 60`), if Coin A is held at weight $24/25$ at price $100$ (leaving $1/25 = 0.04$ cash) and before the next rebalance Coin A rises $+10\%$ to $110$ (or `oracle_price = 110`) and charges the venue's $4\%$ hourly funding cap (`unit_payment = 4.4`), the payment is $0.0096 \times 4.4 = 0.04224 > 0.04$ cash, ending that rule (or on the daily clock, two hours of $+2.5\%$ funding between daily rebalances end it). ## 3. Detailed Check of the Wealth at the Bid ($W^b$)

### 3.1 Any path on which $W^b > W$ or a held coin's bid price is undefined?
**None.** On every admitted market and at every day's end $T$:
- By Law K1 ([kernel-v2.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/kernel-v2.md#L348)), before the ledger ends, cash $C_T \ge 0$ and units $u_{c,T} \ge 0$ for every coin $c$.
- For every coin $c$ with $u_{c,T} > 0$, its bid price is $p^b_{c,T} = \min(\text{bid}_{c,T}, \text{price}_{c,T}) \le \text{price}_{c,T}$. Thus:
  $$W^b_T = C_T + \sum_{c : u_{c,T} > 0} u_{c,T} \min(\text{bid}_{c,T}, \text{price}_{c,T}) \le C_T + \sum_{c : u_{c,T} > 0} u_{c,T} \text{price}_{c,T} = W_T.$$
- All four boundary cases hold:
  1. *A coin with no quote yet before $T$:* By A3 ([kernel-v2.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/kernel-v2.md#L52)) and A7 ([kernel-v2.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/kernel-v2.md#L131)), a coin is stale before its first quote and no order can arrive or fill before its first quote. Thus $u_{c,T} = 0$, and `confirm` needs no `bid` for unheld coins.
  2. *A listing's end in a day's last millisecond ($t = T - 1$):* At $t = T - 1$, A12 ([kernel-v2.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/kernel-v2.md#L180)) closes the account's entire position in that coin at the settlement price and emits a `flow` row (`what = settlement`) at $t = T - 1 < T$. By K1, the test ledger sells all of its units of that coin at $t = T - 1$, so at midnight $T$ the ledger holds $u_{c,T} = 0$ of the ended coin (which has left the new span's `at = day` rows at $T$).
  3. *A span that ends at a midnight ($t = T$):* By [KERNEL.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r2/KERNEL.md#L278), Day $T$'s `at = day` rows stand *before* any event at $T$. Thus if a listing ends at $t = T$, the coin is still in the span open at Day $T$'s `at = day` rows (with its `bid` and `price` from `< T`), and settles at $t = T$ *after* Day $T$'s rows.
