### 1. The Re-derivation Test

#### Is this a genuine re-derivation at the cause, or another representation of the same design?
**It is a genuine re-derivation at the cause.** In Rounds 2, 3, and 4, the test wealth $V$ was a normalized return index (`V ← V × (1 + φ · pnl / a)`) whose numerator and denominator updated on separate clocks (`step`, `quote`, `tick`). Because changing the denominator $a$ rescaled exposure to already-held units without paying the current price for that trade, every fix that moved `B°` or deferred fills merely shifted the clock mismatch one level down.

By replacing $V$ with a **self-financing test ledger** ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L261-L276), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L192-L198)) that holds its own non-negative cash $C_t \ge 0$ and coin units $U_{c,t} \ge 0$, values every coin at one price $P_{c,t}$ (latest quote mid or print since, [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L51)), and changes units $U_{c,t}$ *only* when the judged account executes a fill at that fill's price and fee, Fable eliminates the separate stake clock, the step clock, `at_risk`, `fundable`, and deferred fill counting at their root:
- Between fills of the account, $U_{c,t}$ is constant; at any price event of coin $c$ with return $r_{c,t}$, the test ledger's wealth $W_t = C_t + \sum_{c'} U_{c',t} P_{c',t}$ multiplies by $1 + w_{c,t^-} r_{c,t}$ with predictable weight $w_{c,t^-} = \frac{U_{c,t^-} P_{c,t^-}}{W_{t^-}} \in [0, 1]$, on the exact same event clock as every constant mix $1 + b_c r_{c,t}$.
- At any fill of the account, buying $\Delta u \ge 0$ units at $p_{\text{fill}}(1+f)$ changes $W_t$ by $\Delta u(P_{c,t} - p_{\text{fill}}(1+f)) \le 0$ and selling $\Delta u \ge 0$ units at $p_{\text{fill}}(1-f)$ changes $W_t$ by $\Delta u(p_{\text{fill}}(1-f) - P_{c,t}) \le 0$, provided Law `T1 ★` holds ($p_{\text{fill}} \ge P_{c,t}$ on buys, $p_{\text{fill}} \le P_{c,t}$ on sells).

#### Judgment on Each Row of Fable's Table ([fable-report-5.md](audit/program/fable-report-5.md#L25-L29))

1. **Row 1 — A benchmark taken for a null ([fable-report-5.md](audit/program/fable-report-5.md#L27)):**
   - *Why it cannot return in the old form:* **Sound.** Units $U_{c,t}$ cannot change without a fill, and both $W$ and the constant mix $B(b)$ step one coin at a time at every price event.
   - *Where it still could return:*
     - On **Rung 2's null and guard (D30, O4)**: Fable rightly concedes that when one price event predicts the next (negative cross-serial correlation), a rebalancer outgrows every event-clock constant mix, and withdraws the name `"timed"`. However, Fable's pre-freeze guard ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L353): registering Rung 2 iff $\bar{B}$ exceeds the best 1-hour and 1-day mixes) **does not catch sub-hourly lead-lag**—including our exact Round-4 construction when pairs occur faster than 1 hour (**Finding 3** below).
     - On **Rung 1's null under bouncing prints (O3)**: Fable's argument that a long-only account without maker fills cannot compound gains from bid-ask print bounces **holds** (see **Attack 3** below), subject to fixing `T1 ★` on short liquidations and listing settlements (**Finding 2**).
2. **Row 2 — A state taken for a flow ([fable-report-5.md](audit/program/fable-report-5.md#L28)):**
   - *Why it cannot return in the old form:* **Sound.** Every fill, fee, funding payment, liquidation close, and absorption is booked at its own event.
   - *Where it still could return (each open cycle's unspent budget):* In the **exact** rational ledger, `unspent_budget` is an internal reservation of cash $C_t$; returning it at a cycle's end does not change $C_t$ or $U_{c,t}$, so it affects only future buy scales. **However**, two edge cases in its specification fail (`O5` / **Finding 1**): (i) when funding reduces $C_t$ below open cycles' `unspent_budgets`, `free_cash = cash − unspent_budgets` goes **negative** if not floored at $0$, producing a negative scale on another coin's first buy; and (ii) in `K1 ★`'s **128-bit rounded confirmer ledger** ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L340)), rounding `unspent_budget` and `units` *down* increases `free_cash` and reduces positive funding liabilities, which can make the confirmer's wealth exceed exact wealth or survive a funding event that ended the exact ledger (**Finding 1**).
3. **Row 3 — The account taken for the test ([fable-report-5.md](audit/program/fable-report-5.md#L29)):**
   - *Why it cannot return in the old form:* **Sound.** The test ledger never reads the account's equity, cash, or `absorbed`.
   - *Where it still could return (borrowing cycle end, sale fraction, and never-short from the account):* **Sound in exact arithmetic.** Borrowing when a cycle ends only releases `unspent_budget` inside $C_t$; borrowing the sale fraction $\alpha = \Delta q / q_{\text{before}} \in [0, 1]$ sells $\alpha U_{c,t} \ge 0$ units at $p_{\text{fill}}(1-f) \le P_{c,t}$, which cannot raise $W_t$; and stopping when a fill takes the account short is an adapted stopping time.

#### Do our Round-4 constructions still work against it in any form?
- **Round-4 Finding 1.1 (held-lot dilution via fills/wishes after a print) and GPT's Finding 1:** **Completely defeated.** Because a print immediately moves the coin's price $P_{c,t}$ and neither wishes nor fills on any coin can rescale the units $U_{c,t^-}$ already held, the held lot takes the full print move before any fill is booked, and the new buy at $p_1 = 99.99 > 99$ loses $p_1 - P_{c,t}$ on entry.
- **Round-4 Finding 2 and GPT's Findings 2–3 (uncounted sales and bankruptcy closes across steps):** **Completely defeated.** Every sale and liquidation close is booked in cash and units at its own event.
- **Round-4 Finding 1.2 (alternating quotes of two anti-correlated coins):** **Conceded by Fable on the test, and STILL defeats the new §6.5 guard ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L353)).** When the two-coin pairs occur inside an hour (e.g., every 5 minutes), the coins' returns at the 1-hour and 1-day clocks are equal ($1.25 \times 0.85 = 1.0625$ per pair-pair), so the event-clock bar $\bar{B}$ is strictly higher than both the 1-hour and 1-day mixes ($\bar{B} > B_{\text{hour}}^\circ = B_{\text{day}}^\circ$, **passing the §6.5 guard**), while a rebalancer on the pair clock still grows by $1.05$ per pair vs $1.040625$ and passes `"outgrew"` with probability $1$ (**Finding 3**).

### 2. Adversarial Attacks (in the Ledger's Order)

#### Finding 1 (Attack 1 — The Test Ledger, D29 & O5): Downward 128-bit rounding of stored numbers in `K1 ★` reduces positive funding payments and `unspent_budget` (letting the confirmer outgrow or outlive the exact ledger); funding can drive `free_cash` and scales negative; and `K1 ★` promises "wealth at any event" from rows that carry all coins' prices only at day's end
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result (a run whose exact test ledger ended at a funding event can still be confirmed `"earned"` and `"outgrew"` by the 128-bit confirmer; a multi-coin run after funding can get a negative scale), and Lean proof gate (`K1 ★` in [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L340) is unprovable as stated).
- **The claim:**
  1. [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L340) (`K1 ★`) claims: *"For every stake rule, with scales that are not negative: the ledger's cash and units are never negative before its end; it changes only at a cash-flow row; and its wealth at any event, valued at that event's prices, is the same whether folded over every event of the run or over the rows alone. The confirmer's ledger is that fold with each stored number rounded down to 128 significant bits, so it is at most the exact one and at least it less `2⁻¹⁰⁰` of itself over at most `2²⁴` rows."*
  2. [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L271) claims that setting aside $\phi$ of `free_cash = cash − sum(unspent_budgets)` at each cycle's first buy gives non-negative scales.
- **Concrete constructions:**
  1. **Rounding stored `units` $U_c$ and `unspent_budget` $B_c$ *down* to 128 significant bits violates both sides of `K1 ★`:**
     - **Why rounding $U_c$ down can make the confirmer survive and confirm when the exact ledger ended (or make $W_{\text{conf}} > W_{\text{exact}}$):**
       - At a buy of Coin A where $\Delta u = \text{spend} / (p(1+f))$ is not a dyadic rational (e.g., $p = 100$, maker fee $f = 0.00015$, $\text{spend} = 1/2$), rounding the stored unit balance $U_A$ down to 128 significant bits gives $U_{A,\text{conf}} = U_{A,\text{exact}} - \epsilon$ with $\epsilon \in (0, 2^{-128} U_A]$.
       - At a later positive funding payment ($\rho > 0$, longs pay), the test ledger pays $F = U_A \cdot O \cdot \rho$ from its cash $C$ ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L195)). Because $U_{A,\text{conf}} < U_{A,\text{exact}}$, **the confirmer's ledger pays strictly less funding than the exact ledger**: $F_{\text{conf}} = F_{\text{exact}} - \epsilon O \rho < F_{\text{exact}}$.
       - *False survival / false confirmation:* Suppose before this funding event, exact cash is $C_{\text{exact}} = F_{\text{exact}} - \frac{1}{2}\epsilon O \rho$ (with $C_{\text{conf}} = C_{\text{exact}}$). Then in the **exact** ledger, $C_{\text{exact}} < F_{\text{exact}}$, so **the exact ledger ends** ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L195)) and can never confirm. In the **confirmer's** ledger, $F_{\text{conf}} = F_{\text{exact}} - \epsilon O \rho < C_{\text{conf}}$, so **the confirmer's ledger does not end**, survives, and confirms `"earned"` when Coin A subsequently rallies $40\times$!
       - *Conversely, premature ending (violating the $1 - 2^{-100}$ lower bound):* Suppose $U_A = 1$ is dyadic (no unit rounding error), while $C_{\text{conf}} = C_{\text{exact}} - \epsilon$ was rounded down at the buy. At a funding payment of $F = C_{\text{exact}}$, the exact ledger has $C_{\text{exact}} - F = 0 \ge 0$ (survives and reaches $W_{\text{exact}} = 25$), while the confirmer's ledger has $C_{\text{conf}} - F = -\epsilon < 0$ and **ends**, failing `K1 ★`'s lower bound.
       - *Wealth inversion ($W_{\text{conf}} > W_{\text{exact}}$ without ending):* If Coin A rallies to $200$, pays four settlements with $\sum O_k \rho_k = 120 > 100$, and then falls to $P_{\text{end}} = 10$, the confirmer saved $120\epsilon$ in funding while losing only $10\epsilon$ in final unit value, giving $W_{\text{conf}} - W_{\text{exact}} = +110\epsilon > 0$.
     - **Why rounding `unspent_budget` down increases `free_cash`:**
       - `free_cash` is `cash − sum(unspent_budgets)` ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L271)). Rounding a stored `unspent_budget` *down* to 128 bits subtracts a smaller number from `cash`, making `free_cash` (and the next cycle's scale) **larger** than in the exact ledger.
  2. **Negative `free_cash` and negative scale when funding reduces `cash` below open cycles' `unspent_budgets`:**
     - In [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L271), at a cycle's first buy on Coin 1 with cash $C_0 = 1$, $\phi = 1$ (as in [arithmetic.py.txt](audit/program/arithmetic.py.txt#L235) and [L307](audit/program/arithmetic.py.txt#L307)) and depth $d = 6$ ($27.633\times$ first buy), the first buy spends $1 / 27.633 = 0.0362$, leaving $C = 0.9638$ and `unspent_budget[Coin 1]` $= 0.9638$. (The same happens for any $\phi < 1$ once cumulative funding exceeds $1 - \phi$.)
     - Coin 1 then pays a funding settlement of $0.001$ from $C$, reducing $C$ to $0.9628$ while `unspent_budget[Coin 1]` remains $0.9638$.
     - Coin 2 now executes its cycle's first buy. By [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L271), *"free cash: its cash less the open cycles' unspent budgets"* is $C - B_1 = 0.9628 - 0.9638 = \mathbf{-0.001 < 0}$. Without clamping `free_cash` at $\max(0, \dots)$, Coin 2 gets a **negative budget and negative scale**, violating `K1 ★`'s non-negative scale premise.
  3. **`K1 ★`'s statement of "wealth at any event" from the rows alone:**
     - [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L188-L190) emits prices for *all* universe coins only in the UTC day-end rows; a cash-flow row at an intermediate event $t$ carries only *that* event's coin's price, not the prices of other held coins. Thus folding the rows alone determines `cash` and `units` after every event, and determines `wealth` **at each UTC day's end** (as [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L287) and [build-plan.md](audit/program/build-plan.md#L156) Step 10 Test 3 state), not *"at any event"* as `K1 ★` L340 states.
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):**
  1. Since Lean already has exact `Rat` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L278)) and only cash-flow rows are folded (e.g., $5{,}806$ rows in 7 months), either fold the test ledger in exact `Rat` in `confirm`, **or** if 128-bit floating-point is used, maintain directed 128-bit **lower and upper bounds** $(C^{\text{lo}}, U_c^{\text{lo}}, B_c^{\text{hi}})$ and $(C^{\text{hi}}, U_c^{\text{hi}}, B_c^{\text{lo}})$ (charging positive funding on $U_c^{\text{hi}}$ rounded up in the lower bound and on $U_c^{\text{lo}}$ rounded down in the upper bound, and requiring $C^{\text{lo}} \ge 0$ to remain un-ended for confirmation).
  2. Define `free_cash = max(0, cash − sum(unspent_budgets))` in [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L271).
  3. In `K1 ★` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L340)), state that the row fold equals the event fold in cash and units after every event and in wealth at each day's end.

#### Examination of Attack 3 (O3 — Bouncing Prints and Non-Maker Accounts)
- **Verdict on O3 ([ledger.md](audit/program/ledger.md#L665)):** **Fable's argument holds for long-only accounts** (once Finding 2's short-liquidation rule in `A10` is fixed).
- **Why no non-maker can compound the bid-ask print bounce:**
  1. By `A7` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L131)), an order can only take at its **arrival**, which is always a **quote** (`the coin's first quote with time > ℓ`), never a print.
  2. At that arrival quote $q_t$, `A12` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L182)) moves the coin's price $P_{c,t}$ to the quote's mid $M_t = (b_t + a_t)/2$ **before** arrival fills run, and the arriving taker buy pays $a_t(1 + f_{\text{taker}}) > M_t$. Thus, even if the event immediately preceding $q_t$ was a sell print at $b_{t^-}$, the price bounce $b_{t^-} \to M_t$ happens *before* the taker buys and is earned only by units $U_{c,t^-}$ already held before the sell print (which took the equal and opposite move $M_{t^-} \to b_{t^-}$).
  3. Similarly, any taker sale executes at a later quote $q_{t'}$ at $b_{t'}(1 - f_{\text{taker}}) < M_{t'}$, so all intermediate print bounces between $q_t$ and $q_{t'}$ telescope out of $W$ (`K1 ★`).
  4. *Note (non-compounding terminal mark):* Because `K1 ★` checks $W \ge 20$ at UTC day's end using the coin's price (`A3` L51), if the final event before UTC midnight is a buy print at a wide ask $a_{\text{end}} > M_{\text{end}}$, held units are marked at $a_{\text{end}}$ rather than $M_{\text{end}}$ at that day's end. This is a one-time half-spread boundary effect (bounded by $(a_{\text{end}} - M_{\text{end}})/M_{\text{end}}$ at stopping) and cannot be compounded across trades.

#### Finding 3 (Attack 4 — Rung 2's Limit and Guard, D30 & O4): Fable's §6.5 guard ($\bar{B} > B_{\text{hour}}^\circ$ and $\bar{B} > B_{\text{day}}^\circ$) PASSES on our exact Round-4 counterexample whenever pairs occur faster than 1 hour, while a sub-hourly rebalancer still passes `"outgrew"` with probability 1
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result, and Study 2's registration gate ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L353) registers Rung 2 on markets where a constant-weight rebalancer still passes `"outgrew"`).
- **The claim:** [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L293) and [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L353) claim that checking whether the event-clock bar $\bar{B}$ is higher than the best 1-hour mix $B_{\text{hour}}^\circ$ and the best 1-day mix $B_{\text{day}}^\circ$ detects markets whose price events follow each other in the direction that lets a rebalancer pass Rung 2.
- **Concrete construction:**
  - Take our exact Round-4 Finding 1.2 two-coin market ([fable-report-5.md](audit/program/fable-report-5.md#L27), [arithmetic.py.txt](audit/program/arithmetic.py.txt#L363-L370)): in each pair of quotes, Coin 1 and Coin 2 move by $(r_1, r_2) = (+25\%, -15\%)$ or $(-15\%, +25\%)$.
  - Suppose pairs occur every **5 minutes** (12 pairs per hour, e.g., alternating $(+25\%, -15\%)$ and $(-15\%, +25\%)$, or i.i.d. across pairs).
  - **What happens at the 1-hour and 1-day clocks ($B_{\text{hour}}^\circ$ and $B_{\text{day}}^\circ$)?**
    - Over any pair of opposite pairs (10 minutes), **both** Coin 1 and Coin 2 multiply by the exact same factor:
      $$(1 + 0.25)(1 - 0.15) = 1.0625 \quad (\text{i.e., } \sqrt{1.0625} = \mathbf{1.030776}\text{ per pair}).$$
    - Therefore, at the **1-hour clock** (6 pair-pairs) and at the **1-day clock** (144 pair-pairs), Coin 1 and Coin 2 have identical cumulative returns ($1.0625^6 - 1 = +43.86\%$ per hour), so **neither the 1-hour mix nor the 1-day mix earns any rebalancing bonus**:
      $$B_{\text{hour}}^\circ = B_{\text{day}}^\circ = 1.030776\text{ per pair} \quad (1.4386\text{ per hour}).$$
  - **What happens at the event-clock mix $B_{\text{event}}^\circ$ and the certified bar $\bar{B}$?**
    - At every price event, the best constant mix $b = (1/2, 1/2)$ multiplies by $(1 + 0.25/2)(1 - 0.15/2) = 1.125 \times 0.925 = \mathbf{1.040625}$ per pair ($1.6127$ per hour), and the certified cubic bar $\bar{B}$ is $\mathbf{1.04199}$ per pair ($1.6383$ per hour).
    - Thus:
      $$\bar{B} = 1.6383 > B_{\text{event}}^\circ = 1.6127 > B_{\text{hour}}^\circ = B_{\text{day}}^\circ = 1.4386\text{ per hour!}$$
      **The market passes Fable's §6.5 guard ($\bar{B} > B_{\text{hour}}^\circ \ge B_{\text{day}}^\circ$) by $+13.9\%$ per hour!** (Why? Because at the 1-hour and 1-day boundaries, intra-hour positive serial correlation *within* each coin's $(+25\%, -15\%)$ cycle makes $1.0308 < 1.0406$, hiding the 5-minute cross-coin lead-lag from 1-hour and 1-day snapshots.)
  - **What happens to the 5-minute pair-rebalancer's wealth $W$?**
    - The account that rebalances to $50\% / 50\%$ after each 5-minute pair grows by $1 + \frac{1}{2}(0.25) + \frac{1}{2}(-0.15) = \mathbf{1.05000}$ per pair ($1.7959$ per hour), outgrowing $\bar{B} = 1.04199$ per pair by $+0.00766$ of log wealth per pair and crossing $W \ge 20 \bar{B}$ in $33$ hours with probability $1$.
  - **Does a condition on the record exist under which no holding/rebalancer passes?**
    - *Buy-and-hold (never traded after entry):* With zero funding, $W_T \le \max_{c} B(e_c) \le B^\circ \le \bar{B}$ holds **pathwise on every record with no condition at all** ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L290)).
    - *Rebalanced constant-weight holdings at arbitrary clocks:* A constant-weight strategy $b \in \Delta$ rebalanced at times $\tau_0 < \tau_1 < \dots < \tau_K$ has wealth $\prod_{k=1}^K \left(1 + \sum_c b_c R_{c, (\tau_{k-1}, \tau_k]}\right)$. No strategy of this class can exceed $\bar{B}$ if and only if $\sup_{\tau_0 < \dots < \tau_K} \max_{b \in \Delta} \prod_{k=1}^K \left(1 + \sum_c b_c R_{c, (\tau_{k-1}, \tau_k]}\right) \le \bar{B}$—which fails on any path where some sub-interval has either cross-coin lead-lag or single-coin same-sign trends. Checking two fixed clocks (1h, 1d) only tests two partitions $\tau$ out of all sub-hourly/multi-hour partitions.
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** State in [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L293) and [§6.5](audit/program/architecture.md#L353) that the 1-hour / 1-day check is a diagnostic at those two scales (or add the replica's median cycle duration, e.g., 5-minute and 15-minute mixes, to the guard), and rely on the narrowed claim wording (`"outgrew every constant mix, at every price event"`, never `"not a holding's"`) rather than claiming the guard blocks sub-hourly rebalancers.

  1. **A listing end inside a UTC day loses the ending span's final partial day (including the settlement return!) from the report rows (`A12` L189, `K1 ★` L340, `K2 ★` L341):**

#### Examination of Attack 6 (The End of the Test, D32)
- **Does ending at a funding payment that cash cannot pay keep the 5% size?** **Yes.**
  - Let $\tau_{\text{end}}$ be the first event where either a funding bill $F_t = U_{c,t^-} O_t \rho_t > C_{t^-}$ or an account fill takes $q_{c,t} < 0$. Because $\tau_{\text{end}}$ is a stopping time on the event filtration, the stopped process $W_{t \wedge \tau_{\text{end}}}$ is a supermartingale under Rung 1's null (and $W_{t \wedge \tau_{\text{end}}} / B(b)_{t \wedge \tau_{\text{end}}}$ under Rung 2's null).
  - Crucially, under V16 ($O_t \rho_t \le P_{c,t}$, i.e., $r_{c,t} \ge -1$), **even at the event $t = \tau_{\text{end}}$ where cash $C_{t^-} < F_t$ cannot pay the funding bill in cash, the test ledger's total wealth $W_{\tau_{\text{end}}}$ is still non-negative**, because the value of its held units $U_{c,t^-} P_{c,t} \ge U_{c,t^-} O_t \rho_t = F_t$ covers the funding charge:
    $$W_{\tau_{\text{end}}} = C_{t^-} + U_{c,t^-}(P_{c,t} - O_t \rho_t) + \sum_{c' \ne c} U_{c',t^-} P_{c',t} \ge 0.$$
  - Thus $W_{t \wedge \tau_{\text{end}}} \ge 0$ throughout, and Ville's inequality gives $\mathbb{P}(\exists t < \tau_{\text{end}} : W_t \ge 20) \le 1/20 = 5\%$.
- **What if a payment exceeds the unit's price ($O_t \rho_t > P_{c,t}$, so $r_{c,t} < -1$)?**
  - Then $W_{\tau_{\text{end}}}$ can fall below zero by at most $\text{shortfall} = \max(0, -W_{\tau_{\text{end}}})$, bounding the pre-stop confirmation probability by $(1 + \mathbb{E}[\text{shortfall}])/20$ ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L276)), while `K1 ★` refuses confirmation from that day on because `least_return < -1` (once Finding 4.1's cross-span persistence is applied).

- **Concrete construction:**
  1. **Same-millisecond print and quote after a gap $\ge G$ (`A3` L46 vs. `A3` L52):**
     - By `A3` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L46)), *"A print comes before a quote of its own millisecond"*. By `A3` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L52)), a coin is stale from its latest quote's `time + G` until its next quote, and *"While a coin is stale no order of it fills."*
     - Suppose the previous quote was at $t_0 = 0$ and the next quote is at $t_1 = G + 10\text{ ms}$, while a real maker fill's print also has timestamp $t_1 = G + 10\text{ ms}$.

- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** In [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L337), specify that Layer 2's join evaluates staleness at the print's exact position in `A3`'s event order (requiring a quote with `t_print − G ≤ t_quote < t_print`, strict inequality at $t_{\text{print}}$ because prints precede same-millisecond quotes) and checks that the matched print's size is at least the sum of the vault's maker fills matched to it.
