### 2. Adversarial Findings

#### Finding 1 (Rung 2, Nesting, and `K2 ★`): Zero-timing rules with no fill better than mid reach $V \ge 20 B^\circ$ in $>90\%$ of paths, and `K2 ★` (the AM-GM certificate) is false when funding makes $1 + r_{c,d} < 0$
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result (inflates Rung 2 confirmation above $5\%$ under zero return-timing skill, misses valid intermediate-day confirmations, and makes `K2 ★` unprovable in Lean).
- **Confusion landed on:** **C-III (*a benchmark taken for a null*).** Replacing the equal-weight 5-minute basket `B` with the best constant daily mix in hindsight `B°` ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L277-L285)) still substitutes a restricted benchmark class (weights fixed across the entire 2-year horizon and rebalanced only once a day) for the null of "no knowledge of when."
- **The claim:** [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L277-L285) (§6.1), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L303) (`K2 ★`), and [build-plan.md](audit/program/build-plan.md#L141-L150) (Step 10) claim that: (i) in a market where no rule knows when future returns are higher and no fill is better than a mid, $V$ reaches $20 B^\circ$ at most $5\%$ of the time; (ii) Rung 2's rejection implies Rung 1's on every report; and (iii) for a proposed mix with daily factors $f_d$, bounding every mix's wealth by $\left(\prod_{d=1}^t f_d\right) \cdot \left(\max_j \frac{1}{t}\sum_{d=1}^t \frac{g_{d,j}}{f_d}\right)^t$ via AM-GM proves `K2 ★` over rationals.
- **Concrete constructions:**
  1. **`K2 ★`'s AM-GM certificate is mathematically false when funding makes a coin's daily gross factor $1 + r_{c,d} < 0$ ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L279), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L303), [build-plan.md](audit/program/build-plan.md#L150)):**
     - By [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L279), coin $c$'s daily return in `B°` is $r_{c,d} = \frac{\text{mid}_{c,d} - \text{mid}_{c,d-1} - F_{c,d}}{\text{mid}_{c,d-1}}$, where $F_{c,d} = \sum_{h \in d} O_{c,h} \rho_{c,h}$ is the dollar funding a unit position paid during day $d$. Thus the coin's daily gross factor is:
       $$g_{d,c} = 1 + r_{c,d} = \frac{\text{mid}_{c,d} - F_{c,d}}{\text{mid}_{c,d-1}}.$$
     - Because funding is charged hourly on $O_{c,h}$ before the coin's price falls later in the day, $\text{mid}_{c,d} - F_{c,d}$ can be **strictly negative** on a legal run that never violates `V16`: e.g., at the start of day $d$, $\text{mid}_{c,d-1} = 100$; at hour 1, $O_{c,1} = 100$ and $\rho_{c,1} = 0.02$ ($\le 0.04$), so $F_{c,d} = 2$; the coin then drops to $\text{mid}_{c,d} = 1$ by the end of day $d$, giving $g_{d,c} = 1 + r_{c,d} = \frac{1 - 2}{100} = -0.01 < 0$ (or $-2$ if $O_{c,1}$ spiked to $5{,}000$ inside the day before crashing to $1$).
     - When $g_{d,c} < 0$, the AM-GM inequality $\prod_{d=1}^t x_d \le \left(\frac{1}{t}\sum_{d=1}^t x_d\right)^t$ is **false**: on $t = 2$ days with $g_{1,c} = -2$, $g_{2,c} = -2$, and proposed mix $b_0 = \text{cash}$ ($f_1 = f_2 = 1$), the maximum corner mean ratio is $\max\!\left(\frac{-2 + (-2)}{2},\, \frac{1 + 1}{2}\right) = \max(-2, 1) = 1$, so the certificate claims $B^\circ_2 \le 1 \cdot 1^2 = 1$, whereas the corner mix $b = e_c$ has $B_2(e_c) = (-2)(-2) = 4 > 1$! If $V_2 = 25$, the confirmer reports `"timed"` while $V_2 = 25 < 20 \times B_2(e_c) = 80$, **falsifying `K2 ★`**.
  2. **Step-level compounding of `V` vs. daily rebalancing of `B°` on IID steps with zero trades after $t = 0$ ([architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L265-L283)):**
     - Over the $m$ steps of day $d$, $V$ compounds $\prod_{s=1}^m \left(1 + \phi \frac{\text{pnl}_s}{a_{s-1}}\right)$, which **rebalances the test wealth's stake at every step $s$**, whereas $B(b)$ multiplies by $1 + b \cdot r_d$ **once per day**.
     - Consider 1 coin with $m = 6$ IID steps per day (no autocorrelation, no intraday pattern, zero knowledge of when), where each step return $r_{d,s}$ is $+100\%$ w.p. $1/2$ and $-60\%$ w.p. $1/2$ ($\mathbb{E}[r_{d,s}] = +20\%$). An account that buys 1 lot at $t = 0$ at mid and never trades again, with $\phi = 1/3$ (or 1 resting buy order of $2\times$ notional far below the market so $\text{pos\_notional}/a = 1/3$), has $\frac{V_d}{V_{d-1}} = \prod_{s=1}^6 (1 + \frac{1}{3} r_{d,s})$ with expected daily log growth $3 \log(16/15) = +0.1936$/day. Meanwhile, the un-rebalanced full-day return $1 + R_d = \prod_{s=1}^6 (1 + r_{d,s})$ has median $(0.8)^3 = 0.512$, and the best daily constant mix $b^* \approx 0.23$ has expected daily log growth $+0.088$/day. Thus $\log(V_d / B^\circ_d)$ grows at $+0.105$/day and crosses $\log 20$ in 30 days on $>99\%$ of paths.
     - Similarly, on $N = 16$ coins with IID step returns and common drift, rebalancing to equal weights across the 16 coins at mid every 5 minutes harvests $288\times$ per day of multi-coin Fernholz/Cover rebalancing growth $\frac{1}{2}(1 - \frac{1}{N})\sigma_{\text{step}}^2$ that daily-rebalanced $B^\circ$ cannot capture.
  3. **Coins leaving (or entering) the universe freeze `B°`'s weights in dead cash (`r = 0`) while `V` stays $100\%$ invested ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L168), [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L279)):**
     - By `A12` L168 and [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L279), a coin whose listing ended keeps its settlement price $P_{\text{settle}}$, so its daily return in `B°` is $r_{c,d} = 0$ on every subsequent day.
     - Suppose 4 coins are active for one 6-month half-year each over the 2-year horizon (Coin $j$ is active in half-year $j$ with IID daily return $+0.6\% \pm 1\%$, then delists at mid at the end of half-year $j$ while the next coin is active). A zero-skill rule that holds the currently active coin at mid has `at_risk` equal to the active coin's notional (since settled delistings move to cash and leave `at_risk`), so $V$ is $100\%$ invested every day ($\mathbb{E}[\log V_{730}] \approx 4.34, V_{730} \approx 77$). Any constant mix $b = (b_1, b_2, b_3, b_4)$ with $\sum b_j \le 1$ has at least $3/4$ of its weight in dead cash ($r = 0$) on average across the 4 half-years ($\max_b \log B_{730}(b) \approx 1.09, B^\circ_{730} \approx 2.97$), so $V_{730} / B^\circ_{730} \approx 25.9 \ge 20$ on $>90\%$ of paths.
     - Furthermore, if a coin delists at $P_{\text{settle}} = 0$ on day $d$ (worthless delisting), then on day $d+1$ its start-of-day mid is $\text{mid}_{c,d} = 0$, causing $r_{c,d+1} = (0 - 0 - 0)/0$ in [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L279) to divide by zero; and if a coin is stale at $w_{\text{start}}$ (`before its first quote`, `A3` L48), its day-1 start mid is undefined.
  4. **Clustered volatility under constant expected return ($\mathbb{E}[r_d \mid \mathcal{F}_{d-1}] \equiv \mu > 0$, zero return timing):**
     - Suppose a coin has constant conditional daily mean $\mathbb{E}[r_d \mid \mathcal{F}_{d-1}] = \mu = +0.25\%$/day on every day (zero predictability of when returns are higher), while observable conditional volatility alternates between calm days ($\sigma_L = 0.8\%$, $80\%$ of days) and turbulent days ($\sigma_H = 30\%$, $20\%$ of days, unconditional $\bar{\sigma}^2 = 0.01805$). Any constant daily mix $b$ suffers variance drag across turbulent days ($\max_b g(b) \approx \mu^2 / (2\bar{\sigma}^2) = 0.00017$/day, $B^\circ_{730} \approx 1.13$). A rule that holds at mid on calm days and is flat (`at_risk = 0`) on turbulent days has $\mathbb{E}[\log V_{730}] = 730 \times 0.80 \times (\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma_L^2) = 1.44$ ($V_{730} \approx 4.2$, or $>25$ over 2 years at $\mu = +0.6\%$/day), crossing $20 B^\circ$ on $>95\%$ of paths without forecasting returns.
  5. **Single proposed mix vs. per-day stopping, and day-end vs. step-end misalignment ([build-plan.md](audit/program/build-plan.md#L141), [build-plan.md](audit/program/build-plan.md#L146)):**
     - [build-plan.md](audit/program/build-plan.md#L141) specifies the confirmer as a function from *"a report and **a proposed mix**"* to the certificate *"at every day's end"*. Because the hindsight-optimal mix $b^*_t$ on day $t = 30$ differs from $b^*_{730}$ on day $730$, passing one mix $b_0$ gives a loose certificate on earlier days $t < 730$ and fails to confirm runs that cross $20 B^\circ_t$ at day $t$.
     - Neither `A4` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L52-L54)) nor `A12` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L168)) requires `tick × decide_every` to divide $86{,}400\text{ s}$, so a day's end need not be a step boundary where `V` is defined (breaking Step 10 Test 4); and in `K2 ★`, raising an exact `Rat` corner mean to power $t \le 730$ without an upward-rounded rational step bound overflows into millions of digits.
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** (1) In `B°` and `K2 ★`, floor each coin's daily gross factor at $g_{d,c} = \max(0, 1 + r_{c,d})$ (a limited-liability daily holding cannot fall below 0, which restores $g_{d,c} \ge 0$ and makes AM-GM and `K2 ★` provable in Lean), exclude unlisted/delisted/stale-start coins on days where $\text{mid}_{c,d-1}$ is undefined or post-settlement so weights are normalized over active coins, pass one proposed mix per day (`Array Mix`), and round the certificate's daily ratio upward in `Rat`; (2) require `tick × decide_every` to divide $86{,}400\text{ s}$ (or emit step rows at day boundaries); and (3) state in the Rung 2 criterion that `B°` tests outperformance against *constant daily-rebalanced mixes* (which credits volatility-regime scaling and sub-daily rebalancing alongside return timing), or define `B°` over step returns if step-level rebalancing is to be inside the null.

#### Finding 2 (`Q1 ★` and `V16`): A step can lose arbitrarily many multiples of `at_risk × (1 + ρ̄)` when a cycle resets inside a step, when a cancelled/replaced order fills in `(τ_k, τ_k + λ]`, or when `λ` exceeds a step; and `V16`'s void rule breaks Ville's bound
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result, and Lean proof gate (`Q1 ★` in [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L298) is mathematically false on the transitions of `A5`–`A12` and cannot be proved in `checks.engine-proofs`).
- **Confusion landed on:** **C-I (*a state taken for a flow*)** (`at_risk` treats a slot's start-of-step `remainder` state as a cap on total flow across cycle resets in the step) and **C-II (*the account/floor taken for a bound*)** (`V16` voids runs after a step's funding exceeds $\bar{\rho}$, truncating tail losses just like `A11`).
- **The claim:** [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L168) (`A12`), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L202) (`V16`), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L298) (`Q1 ★`), and [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L261) (§6.1) claim that for an account with no short and no order that can open one, a step's `pnl` plus funding paid is at least $-\text{at\_risk}$ at the step's start (`Q1 ★`), and with funding at most $a = \text{at\_risk}(1 + \bar{\rho})$, so $V$ is never negative on any non-void run (`K1 ★`).
- **Concrete constructions:**
  1. **A cycle reset inside a step resets `filled` to $0$ (`A5` L78) and lets a live/pending order refill its target multiple times in one step, violating `Q1 ★` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L78), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L105), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L168), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L298)):**
     - By `A5` L78, *"A cycle on a coin runs from the first fill while flat to the fill that makes it flat. A slot's `filled` counts within the cycle and resets when it ends."* By `A6` L105, *"The world, not the view, computes what an order may still fill: its remainder is its target minus the slot's fills in the cycle so far, never below zero, and for `close` the position."*
     - Suppose at the start of step $[\tau_k, \tau_{k+1})$, the account holds `pos = 1` lot of a coin at $\text{mid}_0 = 100$. Buy slot 1 has a live `gtc` order at $p = 100$ with `target = 10` lots that has already filled $9$ lots earlier in the cycle (`filled(slot 1) = 9`, after 8 lots were previously reduced, or `pos = 10` and `filled(slot 1) = 9`). Sell slot 2 (`reduce_only = true`, `size = close`) has a live `gtc` order at $p_{\text{sell}} = 100$.
     - At the step's start $\tau_k$, Buy slot 1's remainder is $\text{target} - \text{filled} = 10 - 9 = 1$ lot. Thus start-of-step `at_risk` is:
       $$\text{at\_risk} = 1 \times 100 + 1 \times 100(1 + f_{\text{taker}}) = \$200.045.$$
     - At $t_1 \in (\tau_k, \tau_{k+1})$ (inside the step), a buy-aggressor print at $100$ fills Sell slot 2 for $1$ lot, bringing `pos` to $0$ (`flat`). **The cycle ends, so `filled(slot 1)` resets from $9$ to $0$ (`A5` L78, `S1 ★` L290)!**
     - Buy slot 1's live `gtc` order (which never completed its target of $10$, or which had a replacement pending for $\tau_k + \lambda > t_1$) now has world remainder $\text{target} - \text{filled} = 10 - 0 = 10$ lots!
     - At $t_2 \in (t_1, \tau_{k+1})$ (still inside the same step), the coin crashes to $0$, filling all $10$ lots of Buy slot 1 at $100$ on the way down to $0$.
     - **Step loss:** $\text{pnl} = -\$1{,}000.15 < -\text{at\_risk} = -\$200.045$ (**$5\times$ `at_risk`**, and with $K$ oscillating prints between $100$ and $100.01$ before crashing to $0$, **arbitrarily many multiples $10K \times \$100$ of `at_risk` in a single step**), falsifying `Q1 ★` and driving $V < 0$!
  2. **A cancelled or reduced order filling in $(\tau_k, \tau_k + \lambda]$, and $\lambda$ longer than a step ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L105), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L168)):**
     - By `A6` L105, when a slot's wish changes (or goes inactive) at deciding tick $\tau_k$, *"the live order is cancelled and the wished one placed, both taking effect at `τ + λ`; until then the old order can still fill."*
     - If `at_risk` at the start of step $[\tau_k, \tau_{k+1})$ is computed after $\tau_k$'s reconciliation and records only the slot's new pending wish (e.g., `when = false`, or new price $p_{\text{new}} = 10 < p_{\text{old}} = 100$, or new target $T_{\text{new}} = 1 < T_{\text{old}} = 10$), then in $(\tau_k, \tau_k + \lambda]$ the old live order $O_{\text{old}}$ still fills $10$ lots at $100$ and crashes to $0$, losing $\$1{,}000.45 > \text{at\_risk}$!
     - Furthermore, if $\lambda > \text{step}$ (e.g., $\text{step} = 1\text{ s}, \lambda = 2\text{ s}$), `A6` L105 compares the wish at $\tau_1 = 1\text{ s}$ only with the slot's **live** order (which is still `none` because the order wished at $\tau_0 = 0$ is *pending* until $2\text{ s}$), placing a second pending order without cancelling the first.
  3. **`V16` voids runs on any intra-step entry before funding, fails on oracle spikes $O > \text{mid}_{\text{start}}$, and voiding breaks Ville's bound ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L202), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L302)):**
     - [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L202) (`V16`) states: *"A long pays at most `ρ̄` of **its value at the step's start** ... The confirmer voids a run in which a step's funding exceeds it."* Whenever an account starts a step flat (`long_notional = 0`), fills a buy order inside the step, and then crosses an hourly funding timestamp with $\rho > 0$, it pays positive funding $> \bar{\rho} \times 0 = 0$, **voiding the run**!

  1. **`first_price` and `first_size` (`A5` L90) on split/multi-level first buys shrink the ladder by up to $10\times$:** `A5` L90 defines `first_price, first_size` as *"the cycle's first fill"*. In the vault's 2,019 cycles there are 2,247 `0000` first-buy fills ([facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L133)) because an arriving taker first buy sweeps multiple ask levels (or a maker first buy fills in partial prints), and the vault sizes levels 1–5 from the **first buy order's total size/VWAP** ([facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L145)). If level 1 of the book has $0.1$ lots and level 2 has $0.9$ lots, `A5` L90 sets `first_size = 0.1`, shrinking all 5 ladder slots by $10\times$.
  2. **First-tick inputs (`A4` L52, `A5` L86, Step 4 Test 3) are `Undef` whenever $\delta > 0$:** Tick 0 is at $\tau_0 = w_{\text{start}}$ (`A4` L52), while events in $[w_{\text{start}}, w_{\text{end}})$ have `known = time + δ ≥ w_start + δ > τ_0` when $\delta > 0$. Thus at $\tau_0$, no event is known yet and every coin is stale (`before its first quote`, `A3` L48), making Step 4 Test 3 (*"`high`, `low` and `traded` at the first tick cover the horizon's start to it"*) vacuous or `Undef`.
  3. **Four unstated formulas:** (i) **Partial-close cost reduction (`A5` L89, Step 2 Test 1):** `kernel-v2.md` never specifies that a partial close reduces `cost` proportionally (`cost ← cost × pos_new / pos_old`) so `entry = cost / size` stays invariant; (ii) **`age` (`A5` L90):** whether *"ticks since it"* counts ticks since the first fill's `time` or `known`; (iii) **`free_margin` (`A5` L92):** whether it is adverse-price equity minus initial margin of positions and live/pending orders (including taker fees per `A6` L110); and (iv) **Price grid (`A2` L25, `V1` L189, Step 1 Test 5):** whether `listing`'s price grid is a fixed rational step or `V1`'s 5-significant-figure floating step (which changes by $10\times$ at powers of $10$).
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** Define `first_price` / `first_size` from the cycle's opening slot's cumulative fills (or arrival batch), specify proportional `cost` reduction on partial closes, define `age` and `free_margin` explicitly in `A5`, and specify the exact grid representation in `A2`/`A3`.

### 3. What a Run Looks Like Under Each of the Three Human-Only Assumptions

1. **The reading of "timed" ([ledger.md](audit/program/ledger.md#L502)):**
   - **Choice A (Literal benchmark reading: outgrowing every constant daily mix):** The run evaluates $V_d \ge 20 B^\circ_d$ at each day's end; if crossed, the report states that the staked replica outgrew every constant daily mix of its universe by $20\times$, openly crediting volatility-regime avoidance, step-level rebalancing, and active-universe reallocation alongside return timing.
   - **Choice B (Strict "no knowledge of when expected returns are higher" reading):** Because passive rules under clustered volatility, step-level rebalancing, or delistings can cross $20 B^\circ_d$ without forecasting mean returns (**Finding 1**), a run under this reading requires benchmarking at the step level on active coins (or conditioning on volatility state) so only directional timing can confirm.
2. **Replenishment `V10` ([ledger.md](audit/program/ledger.md#L503), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L137)):**
   - **Choice A (Accept `V10`: instant replenishment between events):** The engine treats every snapshot and print as unaffected by our earlier fills; each print is allotted once across resting orders (`E1 ★`), while arriving taker orders can consume a standing ask across successive snapshots (**Finding 4.1**).
   - **Choice B (Reject `V10`: persistent counterfactual book depletion):** After an order takes or fills $s$ lots at price $p$, the engine subtracts $s$ from recorded ask/bid depth at $\le p$ across subsequent snapshots until the book clears above $p$, reducing simulated fills on rapid re-orders.
3. **Disk for two years of recordings ([ledger.md](audit/program/ledger.md#L504), [architecture.md](audit/program/architecture.md#L423)):**
   - **Choice A (Add ~1.2 TB per copy before `2027-05-20`):** The recorder captures `trades` and `l2Book` for all perps across the full 2-year window (through early 2029), supporting both Study 2's full 24-month replay and a venue-wide holdout for future studies.
   - **Choice B (Keep the 300 GiB volume):** Either recording stops around `2027-05-20` (~4 months after the freeze, ending Study 2 early as *"Inconclusive at the markets/days judged"* with near-zero power), or recording is restricted to Study 2's 17 coins (~150 MB/day, completing Study 2's 2 years within 300 GiB but losing the holdout for all other coins).
