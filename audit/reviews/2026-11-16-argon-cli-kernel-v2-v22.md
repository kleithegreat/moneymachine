### 2. Identifying a match by `(coin, buyer_oid, seller_oid)` across execution mechanics

- **Partial fills:** Cannot wrongly refuse an honest CLOB match or admit a duplicate. When an aggressive order and a resting order cross on the book, standard price-time priority matches $\min(\text{rem}_{\text{buy}}, \text{rem}_{\text{sell}})$ in a single execution, exhausting at least one of the two orders completely. An exhausted order leaves the book and never trades again. A resting order filled in $k$ partial fills against $k$ distinct takers produces $k$ distinct `(coin, buyer_oid, seller_oid)` triples, which step 6 test 10 explicitly admits ([v22-fix.diff](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/v22-r1/v22-fix.diff#L83)).
- **In-place order modifications (`modify`) and TWAP slices:** On Hyperliquid, TWAP child slices are submitted as separate orders, and even if a resting order is modified in-place keeping its `oid`, any previous match against a counterparty order $B$ already exhausted either the resting order or $B$. Only if the venue possessed a mechanism where two orders matched *without* exhausting either (e.g., if a multi-slice parent `oid` were written onto child fills against the same unexhausted resting order) could an honest `(coin, buyer_oid, seller_oid)` repeat. Across the 5,933,983 prints of 2026-10-29 to 10-31, 0 pairs repeated ([report.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/v22-r1/report.md#L37)). If one ever occurs in the remaining 209 days, the check **fails closed before `start`** (escalating a wall to the architect rather than silently dropping honest flow or corrupting a run).
- **Self-trades:** A user crossing their own resting order on the book involves two distinct orders (`buyer_oid != seller_oid`), one `crossed: true` and one `crossed: false`, exhausting at least one of them. It is admitted once as one print, matching public book execution (0 self-trade pairs occurred in the 3-day sample, [v22-fix.diff](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/v22-r1/v22-fix.diff#L175)).
- **Liquidations and ADL (Auto-Deleveraging):**
  - *Book liquidations* execute against resting book orders with distinct maker `oid`s and emit one print per maker match.
  - *Off-book liquidations, ADL, and settlements:* If written as a single row or with both rows `crossed: false` (or both `crossed: true`), the derivation produces no print and counts them in unpaired rows. If the venue ever wrote an off-book transfer (such as ADL or a backstop takeover) as two rows in one block on opposite sides with one `crossed: true` and one `crossed: false` and distinct `oid`s, step 6’s derivation would emit it as a print; D122 ([v22-fix.diff](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/v22-r1/v22-fix.diff#L31)) requires counting all prints by their two rows’ `dir` and sending both lists to the architect before any run is trusted.
- **Admitting a wrong record:** A duplicated or fabricated match in which the stream also alters at least one `oid` passes order-pair uniqueness; this is explicitly named in V22 as *"a pair the chain never made, on two orders of its own"* ([v22-fix.diff](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/v22-r1/v22-fix.diff#L36)).
