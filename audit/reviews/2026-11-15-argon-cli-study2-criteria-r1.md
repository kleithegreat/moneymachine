   - Because $68.3\%$ of the vault's first buys (`0000`) are maker fills ([facts-kernel-v2.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r1/facts-kernel-v2.md#L133)), matching on fills under `optimistic` errs in **both** directions: a real maker order that waits $> 6\text{ min}$ in the queue after a touch is a timing miss, **and** a real maker order that is touched without filling in the queue and later cancelled by the vault produces an `optimistic` fill in the replica with no vault fill at all. However, because `historicalOrders` is capped at 2,000 orders and cannot cover the pre-freeze `< 2026-11-01` history used in Step 3's wall, CS2.3 must stay on fills so Step 3 and CS2.3 use one statistic; if `historicalOrders` is certified for the forward recorder, report the placement match alongside CS2.3 as a diagnostic.
3. **Should CS2.2 be in the basket's units?**
   - **No.** The confirmation wall ([architecture.md](file:///home/kevin/.cache/mm-argon/s2-criteria-r1/architecture.md#L323-L334) §6.3) is a function of $W$'s absolute volatility, not $W$'s volatility relative to the basket. Keeping CS2.2 in $W$'s own units preserves the link to the power table's rows, while reporting the basket's volatility ratio beside it separates market weather from cycle utilization.
4. **Defence against a quote that never stood (joining taker fills to the trade record):**
   - **Do not adopt Fable's taker-fill-to-print join as written.** Unlike Layer 2 (where the vault's real fills were executed on Hyperliquid and therefore *caused* a print on the tape), the replica is a paper simulation that sends no orders. When a paper taker order lifts a genuine standing ask during a quiet minute in which no live trader happens to trade at that price, there is **no print on the tape within `G`**, so Fable's rule would falsely withhold a valid claim (and `"a claim that rests on a fill"` is undefined without specifying a counterfactual rerun). Moreover, as shown in Section 1.1 (Construction A), a corrupt or wide-spread quote at midnight inflates `W` without any taker fill at all. If a guard against a single corrupt snapshot is wanted, require that an arriving taker fill's price or a midnight valuation mid lie within a wide sanity multiple (or be corroborated by an adjacent quote), or value long positions at `min(bid, print)` for confirmation checks.
