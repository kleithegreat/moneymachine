## Blocking findings

### R1. Failed evaluation payload mismatches still permit establishment

**Location:** `gate/agent/campaigns.py:61–68,82–88,169–171`

Successful evaluation payloads are now reconciled correctly: the daemon adds `ok` and `remaining` to the committed result fields (`gate/agent/daemon.py:71–75`), and the comparison removes those additions.

But rejected and aborted evaluations still receive only a **kind comparison**. For example:

- the ledger records an evaluation rejection with `E_PARSE`;
- its daemon log instead records `E_TYPE`;
- both map to `reject`;
- neither enters the successful-evaluation comparison.

The audit and `holds` can therefore remain true. With a filled trial elsewhere, two evaluating agents and cross-agent history, the campaign becomes **established despite mismatched agent/ledger evidence**. Check and abort payload mismatches have the same omission.

**Outcome:** unresolved → established; with another counted campaign, failed → pass.

### R2. Completion markers can establish a ledger whose results no longer support establishment

**Location:** `gate/agent/campaigns.py:212–225`; `gate/agent/campaigns.py:158–179`

There are two independent gaps:

1. **A failed holdout-result reconstruction is discarded.** Any exception from `committed_holdout` or `promote` is swallowed at lines 214–215. If a completion marker exists, that failure does not set `crash`. A malformed committed holdout return blob can therefore produce **established**, not unresolved, while the four agent audits remain intact.

2. **The filled-trial requirement comes from the marker, not the current ledger.** Line 219 takes `trials` directly from `decision.json`. Agent reconciliation checks successful evaluation **disclosure payloads**, not their corresponding `experiments`/`results` rows. Missing search-result rows can leave all those checks passing and the marker saying `trials >= 1`, even when the surviving ledger has no filled trial.

The marker proves that execution reached the completion point; it does not prove that the surviving ledger is complete, consistent, or still contains the asserted trials.

The same swallowed reconstruction failure is also pooled as a known non-promotion when the marker exists (`gate/agent/campaigns.py:638`).

**Outcome:** missing or mismatched ledger evidence → established; potentially failed → pass, plus an incorrect pooled denominator/outcome.

### R3. Promotion recovery shrinks the frozen Holm family

**Location:** `gate/agent/campaigns.py:213`; `gate/gate.py:229–234,245–254`

`committed_holdout` returns only finalists with surviving results. Passing that dictionary directly to `promote` makes those results the entire Holm family, ignoring frozen finalists without results.

Consider two frozen finalists. One committed positive-return result has p = 0.03; the other finalist has no result. Recovery tests a family of one and reports promotion. In the original two-finalist family, the surviving candidate would not pass the first Holm threshold of 0.025—even assigning the missing finalist p = 1 would give adjusted p = 0.06.

On an interrupted batch, the normal gate raises rather than completing that decision (`gate/gate.py:214–216`). Recovery nevertheless invents a promotion from the smaller family.

**Why this matters:** for an interrupted campaign, unresolved → promoted does **not** change the promoted-plus-unresolved total. It **does** change the separately required classes, observed-promotion count and exact pooled bound. This is therefore a reporting/classification blocker, not by itself a failed → pass path.

### R4. The criterion test accepts a concealed second unresolved campaign

**Location:** `criteria/test_stages3to5.py:45–51`; `criteria/test_evidence_guards.py:25–59`

All evidence checks are conditional on the supplied class being `established`. Nothing verifies that a campaign labeled `not established` has intact evidence and no disclosed-session crash.

A concrete accepted summary is:

- eight established campaigns;
- one unresolved campaign;
- one campaign labeled not established whose evidence contains `holds: false`.

The test skips the latter campaign, and the supplied classes pass the arithmetic. Its evidence actually requires unresolved, producing **two unresolved campaigns and a failed verdict**.

The guard mutations exercise evidence defects only while the affected campaign remains established, so they miss this case.

**Outcome:** the stage evidence test can accept failed → pass.

### R5. Directory-based recovery does not extend to required reporting

**Location:** `gate/agent/campaigns.py:649–656,668–678`

Classes now include started directories, but much of the report still comes solely from JSONL rows:

- `n`, `ids` and `counted_promotions` remain row-derived;
- finalists, bankruptcies, aborts, denials, history calls and stop causes are not reconstructed;
- recovered audits are not used for the top-level `audits` field.

Consequently, the new verdict can correctly count ten directories while the same report says `n: 9` and lists nine IDs.

A completed campaign that dies after writing its marker but before recording its result is worse: the report can know `trials > 0`, yet label its effective-N diagnostics **“not applicable: no filled trial”** because its JSONL row lacks `search`. Its finalists and other required reporting can disappear too. This is not an empty search; it is missing reporting after a completed search.

**Outcome:** incorrect started-campaign/count reporting and missing or falsely inapplicable C4.4/v3 reporting. The directory fix prevents the old verdict omission, but does not complete recovery.

### R6. An unreadable completion marker aborts classification instead of yielding unresolved

**Location:** `gate/agent/campaigns.py:216–219,634`

Reading and decoding `decision.json` occurs outside the exception handling. An unreadable or malformed marker raises out of `swarm_evidence`, aborting the entire report comprehension.

That also discards an already reconstructed promotion from this invocation. It does not implement the function’s stated rule that unreadable evidence becomes unresolved, nor produce the required separate classification/reporting of the started campaigns.

**Outcome:** required classification and verdict reporting is lost on an evidence-failure path.
