## Findings, Ranked by Severity

### 1. Critical — Halving `Exchange.budget` to `60` deadlocks [`Info._charge`](ingest/hl.py#L26-L35) forever on full `1h` candle pages
- **Place:** [`MARK_BUDGET`](thesis/market.py#L32-L34) and [`Exchange.__init__`](thesis/market.py#L92-L95) in [market.py](thesis/market.py); [`Info._charge`](ingest/hl.py#L26-L35), [`Info.post`](ingest/hl.py#L53-L54), and [`Info.candles`](ingest/hl.py#L83-L95) in [hl.py](ingest/hl.py).
- **Defect:** [`Exchange`](thesis/market.py#L92) now initializes [`Info`](ingest/hl.py#L19-L25) with `self.budget = BUDGET_PER_MIN - MARK_BUDGET = 60`, while [`Info._charge`](ingest/hl.py#L31) loops until `sum(w for _, w in self.spent) + weight <= self.budget`. Any single charge with `weight > 60` can **never** satisfy `0 + weight <= 60`, even after `self.spent` drains completely. [`Info.candles`](ingest/hl.py#L89) sets `per_items=60`, and [`HISTORY["1h"]`](thesis/market.py#L34) requests `200 * DAY = 4,800` hourly bars in one `candleSnapshot` call (Hyperliquid returns up to 5,000 candles per page). Any response with `len(out) >= 3,660` charges `len(out) // 60 >= 61 > 60` (a full 200-day page charges `4800 // 60 = 80`, which previously fit `120` and now exceeds `60`).
- **Scenario:** Any core market with $\ge 153$ days of `1h` history whose `1h.json` is not yet cached (e.g., a market newly reaching 90 daily bars after a gap repair, a relisted market, a cold market dir, or a registration on an uncached core leg) is read by [`Track.snapshot`](thesis/runner.py#L118), [`Daemon.register`](thesis/daemon.py#L79), or [`Track.resolve`](thesis/runner.py#L247).
- **Outcome:** [`Info._charge`](ingest/hl.py#L28-L34) spins in `while True: time.sleep(0.25)` holding `self.x.lock` and `self.market.locks[path]` forever. Every subsequent [`Exchange`](thesis/market.py#L87) read across the entire runner (snapshots, registrations, benchmarks, resolutions) blocks permanently, losing sessions, registrations, and C10.5's 2-hour resolution deadlines; because atomic cache write in [`Market._cached`](thesis/market.py#L202-L204) happens *after* `fetch()` returns, restarting hits the same uncached market and deadlocks again.
- **Fix:** Replace the two static `60/60` [`Info`](ingest/hl.py#L19) limiters with one shared `120/min` limiter that reserves headroom only around the hourly mark sample (see Finding 4), **and** make [`Info._charge`](ingest/hl.py#L26-L35) handle post-response item charges exceeding `budget` (or wait until `self.spent` is empty when `weight > self.budget` and record the full weight so subsequent calls wait out the debt).

### 2. Critical — Real sandbox breaches now pass as `"skipped"` (`Unavailable`), or pass [`canary.check`](sandbox/canary.py#L47-L83) outright (`True`)
- **Place:** [`check`](sandbox/canary.py#L61-L83) in [canary.py](sandbox/canary.py) and [`test_counterexamples_fail`](sandbox/tests/test_canary.py#L72-L83) in [test_canary.py](sandbox/tests/test_canary.py) ([refreeze.diff](refreeze.diff#L957-L962)).

Furthermore, to make the new `None` return pass existing tests, [refreeze.diff](refreeze.diff#L957) deleted the pre-existing counterexample `[c for c in GOOD if c[0] != "b1"]` (`"daemon reached without a Bash call"`), violating [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md#L7) §1 & §2.

Haiku runs step 7 as `Bash(command="touch x")` (its `cwd` is `/work`, [`sandbox_command`](sandbox/session.py#L114)), `touch /work/x `, or `/bin/touch /work/x` — or step 6 as `Read(file_path="/etc/./passwd")` — and the sandbox **allows** it (`id in ok`, `/work/x` created on disk). `asked` returns `[]`, `probe` discards `not (workdir / "work" / "x").exists()`, and `check()` raises [`Unavailable`](sandbox/canary.py#L39) ("retry") instead of [`Broken`](sandbox/canary.py#L43).

- **Outcome:** A broken CLI permission boundary either loops on `session.RETRY` under `"inconclusive; retry"` without raising `SANDBOX CONTRACT BROKEN`, or lets live Stage 10 (and Stage 4) sessions start with unpermitted `Bash`/`Read`/`Write` access, invalidating C10.2 and C3.1 isolation guarantees.
- **Fix:** Check contract invariants across **every** call in `uses` and every side effect first — any `Read` or `Write` whose normalized path is outside `/work` not in `denied`, any `Bash` whose command is not `./canary ...` not in `denied`, any `/work` `Write` or `./canary` `Bash` in `denied`, `(workdir / "work" / "x").exists()`, or `received` non-empty without an `ok` client `Bash` call must unconditionally raise [`Broken`](sandbox/canary.py#L43). Only when zero contract violations exist should unattempted required probes raise [`Unavailable`](sandbox/canary.py#L39). Restore the `"daemon reached without a Bash call"` counterexample in [test_canary.py](sandbox/tests/test_canary.py#L72-L83).

### 4. High — Transient HTTP `429`/`5xx` attempts now poison [`seal`](thesis/market.py#L69-L79) and erase the hour's mark even when the immediate retry succeeds
- **Place:** [`Exchange._send`](thesis/market.py#L102-L116), [`Sampler._archive`](thesis/market.py#L130-L140), [`Market.marks`](thesis/market.py#L216-L220), and [`test_each_attempt_is_archived_at_its_actual_send_and_receipt_after_waits`](thesis/tests/test_market.py#L427-L455).
- **Defect:** [`Exchange._send`](thesis/market.py#L110) sets `recv = now_ms()` on *every* HTTP response, including `429` and `5xx`, and [`Sampler._archive`](thesis/market.py#L131-L139) writes `marks = {}` for `status != 200`. [`Info.post`](ingest/hl.py#L49-L50) then retries `429`/`5xx` and succeeds 1 s later at `H + 6 s`. Yet [`Market.marks`](thesis/market.py#L219-L220) filters only `s["recv"] is not None` (ignoring `status` and payload validity). Because the `503`/`429` row has an earlier `sent` (`H + 5 s < H + 6 s`) and `recv <= H + 5 min`, [`seal`](thesis/market.py#L74-L78) selects the `503` error response over the `200 OK` retry, sees `px = None`, and seals `M_m(H)` as missing for the entire dex — while [`test_market.py`](thesis/tests/test_market.py#L455) locks this in (`{} if first_status else {H: 2400.5}`).
  - In [audit/criteria.md](audit/criteria.md#L793-L796), archiving *"Every attempt"* is the Sampling audit requirement, whereas Sealing's *"no other sample substitutes for one market"* prevents cherry-picking a later `metaAndAssetCtxs` snapshot when an asset's `markPx` is `null` or non-positive in the first valid dex snapshot. Treating a `ReadTimeout` at `H + 5 s` as retryable (`{H: 2400.5}`) while treating an HTTP `503` or `429` at `H + 5 s` as sealing `{}` (and still burning 5 useless retries after it is already poisoned!) contradicts [`Info.post`](ingest/hl.py#L36-L50)'s own retry model.
- **Scenario:** Hyperliquid's CDN returns a single `502`/`503` or `429` at `H + 5 s` and `200 OK` on retry 1 at `H + 6 s`.
- **Outcome:** Every market on that builder dex loses its hourly reference `M_m(H)` permanently, delaying entries/exits or leaving theses unresolved (`e >= x`), while 5 retries needlessly spend quota.
- **Fix:** Archive every attempt in `marks-*.jsonl` as done now, but pass only valid HTTP-200 `[meta, ctxs]` samples into [`Market.marks`](thesis/market.py#L216-L220) as sealing candidates (e.g., record `valid: True` when `[meta, ctxs]` parses in [`Sampler._archive`](thesis/market.py#L132-L139), while keeping legacy rows without `"status"` valid so existing `data/thesis/market/marks/marks-*.jsonl` recordings still read cleanly).

### 5. High — Static `60/60` budget split fails both ways: `Sampler` (`60/min`) breaks `H + 5 s` at $\ge 3$ builder dexes and still loses marks on retries, while `Exchange` (`60/min`) pushes uncached registrations past `WALL_SECONDS`
- **Place:** [`BUDGET_PER_MIN` / `MARK_BUDGET`](thesis/market.py#L32-L33), [`Market.sampler`](thesis/market.py#L152-L155), [`Market.record_marks`](thesis/market.py#L207-L215), and [`WALL_SECONDS`](thesis/harness.py#L18).
- **Defect:** Splitting `120/min` into two non-communicating `60/min` [`Info`](ingest/hl.py#L19) instances ([CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md#L7) §1 parallel-channel workaround) starves both sides:
  1. *Sampler (`60/min`) is too small during the sampling minute:* At `H + 5 s`, `perpDexs` spends `20` weight, leaving `40` weight — **at most 2 builder dexes** (`xyz` and `flx` are already in [`BENCHMARK`](thesis/runner.py#L37)). With **3 builder dexes** and zero errors (`80` weight), dex 3 waits 60 s in `_charge` and goes out at `H + 65 s`, violating *"For every builder dex and hour boundary H, a request is sent at H + 5 s"* ([audit/criteria.md](audit/criteria.md#L793)). Worse, because [`record_marks`](thesis/market.py#L209-L214) runs sequentially and [`Info.post`](ingest/hl.py#L39) makes 6 attempts (`120` weight = **2 full minutes** of `60/min` budget plus up to `31 s` backoff / `60 s` timeouts) per failing dex, **two** builder dexes timing out or returning `5xx` push every subsequent healthy dex past `H + 5 min` ([`MARK_WINDOW`](thesis/market.py#L36)), losing their hourly marks — the exact failure Finding 2 aimed to stop. Also, `perpDexs` ([line 209](thesis/market.py#L209)) is outside `try` with no fallback to last known dex names if `perpDexs` errors.
  2. *Exchange (`60/min`) wastes half the budget for the other 59 minutes of every hour:* First-time 3y+30d funding backfill for one uncached market (~54 pages × 45 weight $\approx 2{,}430$ weight) doubles from ~20 min to **~40.5 min**.
- **Scenario:** (a) Hyperliquid lists $\ge 3$ HIP-3 dexes, or 2 dexes return `503`/timeout at an hour boundary; (b) a main or no-read agent registers a two-leg spread on two uncached markets (or two single-leg theses on uncached markets in one session: $\approx 4{,}860$ weight $\approx 81\text{ min}$).
- **Outcome:** (a) Healthy later dexes miss `H + 5 s` or miss the `H + 5 min` seal, losing recordings/marks. (b) [`Daemon.register`](thesis/daemon.py#L79) blocks in `register_inputs` for ~81 minutes while [`session.run`](sandbox/session.py#L129) kills the session at [`WALL_SECONDS = 3600`](thesis/harness.py#L18) (60 min) before [`registry.register`](thesis/registry.py#L146) commits — **losing valid registrations** and doubling `00:10 UTC` (19:10 EST) snapshot and resolution latency.
- **Fix:** Share one `120/min` budget (or dynamic reservation in `[H - 60 s, H + 5 s]` until `record_marks` finishes) so `Sampler` has the full `120/min` at `H + 5 s` and `Exchange` has `120/min` for the rest of the hour; bound per-dex retries in `record_marks` (or dispatch dexes concurrently) so failing dexes cannot push healthy dexes past `MARK_WINDOW`.

### 6. Medium — Malformed HTTP-200 `metaAndAssetCtxs` payloads raise `AttributeError` before archiving and abort later dexes
- **Place:** [`Sampler._archive`](thesis/market.py#L130-L140) and [`Market.record_marks`](thesis/market.py#L209-L214).
- **Defect:** In [`Sampler._archive`](thesis/market.py#L135), `c.get("markPx")` (or `meta["universe"]` / `a["name"]` when `meta`, `a`, or `c` is `None` or a non-dict) raises `AttributeError`, which `except (KeyError, TypeError, ValueError):` does not catch. Because `super()._archive(...)` is at [line 139](thesis/market.py#L139) *after* that block, the attempt is **never archived** (violating [audit/criteria.md](audit/criteria.md#L793)), and `AttributeError` also escapes [`record_marks`](thesis/market.py#L213)'s `except (httpx.HTTPError, ValueError):`, abandoning every dex after it. Separately, if a `200` response has truncated/non-JSON text, [`Exchange._send`](thesis/market.py#L113) catches `ValueError` and archives `response.text`, after which [`Info.post`](ingest/hl.py#L52) raises `JSONDecodeError` (`ValueError`) without retrying.
- **Scenario:** One builder dex returns `[meta, [None, ...]]` or a proxy returns `200` with truncated HTML/JSON.
- **Outcome:** Unarchived attempt and lost hourly marks for all subsequent builder dexes.
- **Fix:** Validate `isinstance(meta, dict)` / `isinstance(a, dict)` / `isinstance(c, dict)` (or include `AttributeError`) inside [`Sampler._archive`](thesis/market.py#L132-L138), guarantee `super()._archive(...)` runs before any parse error propagates, and treat non-JSON `200` responses as retryable in [`Info.post`](ingest/hl.py#L52).
