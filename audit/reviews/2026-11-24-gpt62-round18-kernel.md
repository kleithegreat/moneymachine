### Listing ends and reader order

**The half-open window is causal in replay event time, and it still discards relevant evidence.** I accept keeping cash and span state unchanged at `E`. At `τ`, the end examines only stamps strictly below `τ`. The later `known` date governs visibility. Neither fact makes the missing observations evidence of delisting. For example, X’s last book is at 03:40, a copy flags it at 05:10, and a valid X book or settlement is stamped exactly 06:10. The reader waives funding from 04:00, closes X at 06:10 at its old price, and discards the resumption. The released cash can fund a same-millisecond buy of Y that otherwise would be cut by the reserve. That changes CS2.1 or CS2.2. Discarding X’s resumed trading can also change CS2.3. A fresh unflagged copy *before* 06:10 can likewise contradict the end while the silence predicate still passes. The recorder unsubscribes on the flag, so some of the apparent corroboration is caused by the flag itself. End after processing the boundary batch if silence through that batch is the evidence being used, and let contradictory or ambiguous listing evidence hold the horizon unless there is independent evidence of an end. That changes the reader and the run. The old reach and finality query can stay deleted. The `Date` check still fixes host-clock dating only under the truthful venue-date premise.
