#### 1. Exact integer ledger vs. binary64 boundary: directed rounding on binary64 representations shifts exact decimal grid prices and lot sizes by a full tick or lot, and `FloatArray` chunking leaves sizes, rates, and own-state round-trips exposed to floats
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result (study 2 conditional replay, fill counts, and forward confirmer wealth; version 1 `B4.1` and `B4.3` checks).
- **The claim:** [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L23-L28) (A2) and [fable-report.md](audit/program/fable-report.md#L20) claim that because the ledger is integer and binary64 expressions only choose a slot's price (rounded to the price grid away from the market: buy down, sell up) and size (truncated toward zero to lots), floats cannot corrupt a judged number and the accounting laws hold with no numeric caveat.
- **Concrete construction:**
  1. **Binary64 representation error + directed rounding on Fable's own ladder ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L102), A5):** Take a coin with `szDecimals = 2` (1 lot $= 0.01$, 5-sig-fig price grid $= 0.001$ around $10$) and `first_price = entry = 10.000` USD.
     - **Buy slot (`first_price * (1 - 0.0225)`):** Mathematically $10 \times (1 - 0.0225) = 9.775$ USD, which lies on the 5-sig-fig price grid (`V1`, [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L185)). In IEEE-754 binary64, $\text{fl}(0.0225) = 6485183463413514 \times 2^{-58} > 0.0225$, so $\text{fl}(10.0 \times (1.0 - 0.0225)) = 9.77499999999999858 < 9.775$. Rounding a buy **down** to the price grid (`A2`) pushes the order to **$9.774$** (or **$9.77499999$** on $10^{-8}$).
     - **Take-profit sell slot (`entry * 1.0056`):** Mathematically $10 \times 1.0056 = 10.056$ USD, also on the 5-sig-fig grid. In binary64, $\text{fl}(1.0056) = 4528820356087546 \times 2^{-52} > 1.0056$, so $\text{fl}(10.0 \times 1.0056) = 10.05600000000000094 > 10.056$. Rounding a sell **up** (`A2`) pushes the take-profit order to **$10.057$**.
     - **Lot size (`1.93` coins, level 2 in [facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L150)):** Mathematically $1.93$ coins $= 193$ lots. In binary64, $\text{fl}(1.93) = 8691945032833433 \times 2^{-52} \approx 1.92999999999999994$, and $\text{fl}(\text{fl}(1.93) \times 100) = 192.99999999999997 < 193$. Truncating toward zero (`A2`) gives **192 lots** instead of **193 lots**.
     - On a path where the bid touches $9.775$ and bounces to $10.056$ (`b = 10.056`), the true order fills both entry and take-profit while `mm2` misses both, yet all 12 ★ laws pass because [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L261) (`L4 ★`) only checks that a fill is at "the order's price" (the already shifted $9.774$ / $10.057$).
  2. **Four float exit paths, not two, plus `FloatArray` ingest ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L75-L77), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L111), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L225)):** Floats also leave the evaluator as `notional` (turned into size at `slot.price` or `mid`, `A5`) and `weight` (`trunc(weight * equity / price - held)`, `A6`), and own-state inputs (`entry`, `first_price`, `equity`) re-enter the binary64 evaluator from the integer ledger. Meanwhile, `feed` (`B1`) passes all market values in one binary64 `FloatArray`: `A2` rounds recorded prices to the nearest $10^{-8}$ decimal, but specifies no nearest-lot rounding for recorded book sizes (`bid_sz`, `ask_sz`) or funding rates (so a recorded size of `0.29` coins in `FloatArray`, which is $0.28999999999999998$ in binary64, truncates to $28$ lots instead of $29$ lots, causing `book-pessimistic` to miss a $29$-lot trade-through).
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** In `A2`, snap slot prices and sizes to the nearest decimal at $10^{-12}$ relative tolerance before applying directed grid/lot rounding; specify nearest-lot and nearest-rate decimal conversion (with an `E_SIZE`/`E_RATE` check parallel to `E_PRICE`) for recorded sizes and funding rates entering from `Chunk`'s `FloatArray`; and compute `notional -> size` in integer arithmetic inside the ledger after rounding `notional` to integer money.

#### 2. Exact integer ledger contradiction: ★ laws `A1 ★` (Conservation), `A2 ★` (Average entry), and `A3 ★` (Rounding) are mutually inconsistent over integers
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result, and Lean proof build (`checks.engine-proofs` in [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L238) cannot prove `A1 ★`, `A2 ★`, and `A3 ★` without `sorryAx`).
- **The claim:** [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L23-L24) (A2), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L89) (A5), [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L146) (A9), and [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L268-L270) (`A1 ★`, `A2 ★`, `A3 ★`) state that prices are integers in $10^{-8}$ USD, money is integers in $10^{-13}$ USD, nothing in the ledger rounds except fees and funding (at most one unit of money per payment, `A3 ★`), a leg's `entry` (type `price`) is the size-weighted mean of opening fills and a close realizes `size * (price - entry)` (`A2 ★`), each leg is charged funding for its own position (`A9`), and at every event equity at mids equals `starting_equity + realized + unrealized - fees - funding` **exactly**, with legs' profits, fees, and funding summing **exactly** to the account's (`A1 ★`).
- **Concrete construction:**
  1. **Average entry and partial/full closes (`A1 ★` + `A2 ★` + `A3 ★`):** A leg opens $q_1 = 1$ lot at $p_1 = 100 \times 10^8$ and $q_2 = 2$ lots at $p_2 = 101 \times 10^8$ (total cost $302 \times 10^8$ price-lots). The size-weighted mean price is $\text{entry} = \frac{302}{3} \times 10^8 \notin \mathbb{Z}$.
     - If `entry` is an integer `price` ($10066666667$), closing all $3$ lots at $p_3 = 102 \times 10^8$ realizes $3(p_3 - \text{entry}) = 399999999$ price-lots while cash increased by $3 p_3 - 302 \times 10^8 = 400000000$ price-lots, violating `A1 ★` and `A3 ★` by $10^5$ units of money (at `szDecimals = 0`).
     - Even if `entry` is held as a rational in $\mathbb{Q}$, a partial close of $1$ lot out of $3$ at $p_3 = 102 \times 10^8$ realizes $\frac{4}{3} \times 10^8$ price-lots $\notin 10^{-13}\mathbb{Z}$, and any quote with `bid + ask` odd in $10^{-8}$ USD makes `mid = (bid + ask)/2` a half-integer in $10^{-8}$ USD.
  2. **Multi-leg funding (`A1 ★` + `A3 ★` + `A9`):** In Study 2's two-leg cross account, let Leg 1 hold $q_1 = +1$ lot and Leg 2 hold $q_2 = -1$ lot on the same coin (net account position $q = 0$), and let a funding event have exact product $1 \cdot O \cdot \rho = 1.5 \times 10^{-13}$ USD. By `A9`, the account's net position $q = 0$ pays $0$. By `A2` and `A9`, Leg 1 (paying) rounds up to $-2$ units of money and Leg 2 (receiving) rounds down to $+1$ unit of money; the legs' funding sum is $-1 \neq 0$, violating `A1 ★` and `A9`. Similarly, for $q_1 = +1, q_2 = +1$ (net $q = +2$) and $O \cdot \rho = 1.4 \times 10^{-13}$ USD, per-leg rounding gives $\lceil 1.4 \rceil + \lceil 1.4 \rceil = 4 \neq \lceil 2.8 \rceil = 3$, violating either `A1 ★` (legs sum to account) or `A3 ★` (at most one $<1$-unit rounding per funding payment).
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** Make `money` an integer count of $2 \times 10^{-14}$ USD (or $10^{-14}$ USD so `mid = (bid + ask)/2` times a lot is always exact integer money); track a leg's open cost basis `cost_money : Int` in money rather than integer `entry`, defining a close of $q_{\text{close}}$ out of $q_{\text{open}}$ lots to realize $q_{\text{close}} \cdot p_{\text{close}} - \lfloor \text{cost\_money} \cdot q_{\text{close}} / q_{\text{open}} \rfloor$ (reducing `cost_money` by that integer amount so conservation `A1 ★` is an identity); and charge funding per leg (`A3 ★` allowing one $<1$-unit rounding per leg's funding payment, with the account's funding defined as the sum of the legs').

#### 4. The Doob threshold $20(1 + \bar{\varepsilon})$ for a gap shortfall is algebraically invalid for a compounding account, and $\bar{\varepsilon}$ is unbounded on short hedges across data gaps
- **Outcome changed:** Judged result (inflates Study 2's forward confirmer Type-I error above 5%).
- **The claim:** [fable-report.md](audit/program/fable-report.md#L61) (D11) claims that for a single cross account whose wealth $W_t$ is its equity ($W_0 = 1$) and whose test ends "not confirmed" at the first liquidation or bankruptcy, Doob's inequality prices a relative gap shortfall $\bar{\varepsilon}$ at the stopping threshold $B = 20(1 + \bar{\varepsilon})$ (e.g., $B = 21$ for $\bar{\varepsilon} = 0.05$).
- **Concrete construction:**
  1. **Compounding scales the dollar gap shortfall with pre-gap wealth $W_{\tau_{\text{liq}}-1}$, breaking $20(1 + \bar{\varepsilon})$:** Because the cross account sizes positions as a fraction of current equity $W_{t-1}$ ([fable-report.md](audit/program/fable-report.md#L63), D13), a gap that breaches zero equity by a fraction $\bar{\varepsilon}$ of current equity from $W_{t-1} < B$ produces an unfloored terminal equity $W_{\tau_{\text{liq}}} = -\bar{\varepsilon} W_{\tau_{\text{liq}}-1}$ (dollar shortfall up to $\bar{\varepsilon} B$, not $\bar{\varepsilon} W_0 = \bar{\varepsilon}$).
     - Consider a null martingale starting at $W_0 = 1$ that at each step $k = 1, \dots, K$ either steps up by factor $r = B^{1/K}$ with probability $\frac{1 + \bar{\varepsilon}}{r + \bar{\varepsilon}}$ or gaps into liquidation at $W_k = -\bar{\varepsilon} W_{k-1}$ (a relative shortfall of $\bar{\varepsilon}$ of pre-gap equity, ending the test "not confirmed") with probability $\frac{r - 1}{r + \bar{\varepsilon}}$, so $\mathbb{E}[W_k \mid \mathcal{F}_{k-1}] = W_{k-1}$.
     - As $K \to \infty$, the probability of reaching $B = 20(1 + \bar{\varepsilon})$ before liquidation is:
       $$\mathbb{P}(\text{claim}) = \lim_{K \to \infty} \left(\frac{1 + \bar{\varepsilon}}{B^{1/K} + \bar{\varepsilon}}\right)^K = B^{-\frac{1}{1 + \bar{\varepsilon}}}.$$
     - At $\bar{\varepsilon} = 0.05$ and $B = 21$, $\mathbb{P}(\text{claim}) = 21^{-1/1.05} = \mathbf{5.51\% > 5\%}$ (and in a discrete 2-step martingale jumping $1 \to 20.99 \to 21$ with $5\%$ shortfall of $W_{t-1}$ at $t=2$ only, Optional Stopping $1 = 21 p - 0.05(20.99)(1-p)$ gives $p = \frac{2.0495}{22.0495} = \mathbf{9.30\% > 5\%}$). At a gap shortfall $\bar{\varepsilon} = 0.50$, Fable's $B = 20(1 + 0.50) = 30$ yields $\mathbb{P}(\text{claim}) = 30^{-2/3} = \mathbf{10.36\% > 5\%}$; the exact threshold for a step-wise relative shortfall $\bar{\varepsilon}$ is $B = 20^{1 + \bar{\varepsilon}}$ ($20^{1.05} = 23.23$ at $\bar{\varepsilon} = 0.05$, $20^{1.5} = 89.44$ at $\bar{\varepsilon} = 0.50$).
  2. **$\bar{\varepsilon}$ is not bounded a priori in a cross account with a short hedge across gaps `G`:** Under [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L45) (A3), the machine does nothing inside a gap, and [facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L116) (§6) records a 183-hour (6-day) archive gap across all 17 coins (`2026-07-12` to `2026-07-17`). A short perp hedge leg has unbounded loss on a rally across a gap, so no theorem bounds $\bar{\varepsilon}$.
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** Separate the **physical replica account** (run at the vault's fixed leverage to produce step returns $r_t$) from the **statistical test wealth $W_t$**, or if compounding inside one account with relative gap-shortfall bound $\bar{\varepsilon}$ per step, use the exact supermartingale threshold $B = 20^{1 + \bar{\varepsilon}}$ (equivalently, stake $\frac{1}{1+\bar{\varepsilon}}$ of equity) and cap maximum gross leverage so $\bar{\varepsilon}$ is bounded for any price jump within a certified maximum gap $G$.

#### 5. Study 2's cross-account dollar-neutral design (`D11`–`D13`) breaks dollar-neutrality under the $10 minimum order rule (`A6`) and fails initial-margin admission (`A6`, `A10`, `V11`) when volatility-scaled
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result (study 2 confirmer either leaks passive market drift $\mu_m$ into the tested series or refuses/liquidates the ladder under `A6`/`A10`).
- **The claim:** [fable-report.md](audit/program/fable-report.md#L61-L64) (`D11`–`D13`) and [human-answers.md](audit/program/human-answers.md#L7) claim that one cross account with a second leg shorting the equal-weight universe is dollar-neutral at every tick (holding a conditional-mean null under unknown market drift) while scaling sizing as a fraction of equity by trailing volatility ($b = 2$ for Sharpe 2) without liquidating the replica.
- **Concrete construction:**
  1. **The $10 minimum order rule ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L111), A6) blocks the 16-coin equal-weight hedge on small ladder fills:** When the ladder leg opens a first buy of notional $N_0 \le \$159$ on one coin (e.g., $\$100$ on a $\$2,500$ or $\$10,000$ account, [facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L17)), an equal-weight short hedge across the 16 archive coins requires a short order of $N_0 / 16 \le \$9.94 < \$10$ per coin. Because these orders are opening/adjusting shorts (not exact `reduce_only` closes), `A6` **refuses all 16 hedge orders**! Since 78% of the vault's 2,019 cycles have only 1 buy order ([facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L143)), the account remains **100% unhedged** on 78% of cycles, allowing a bull market ($\mu_m > 0$) to confirm a zero-skill dip-buyer. Even on larger fills, `A5`'s causality delay ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L100): fill at $t$ is known at $t + \delta$, hedge order placed at $\tau \ge t + \delta$ arrives after $\tau + \lambda$) leaves every fill unhedged for at least $\delta + \lambda$. (Moreover, in `A5` L79, a `market` slot's `filled` resets only when its leg goes flat on that coin, so a continuous 16-coin hedge leg never resets `filled` and can only be written with `target` slots.)
  2. **Equating physical cross-account equity with test wealth (`D11` + `D13`) violates initial-margin admission (`A6`, `V11`):** At $1\times$ vault sizing, the dollar-neutral portfolio has residual annualized volatility $\sigma_{\text{DN}} \approx 12.5\%$. Reaching $200\%$ annualized wealth volatility ($b = 2.0$, needed for the 0.47 / 0.77 power rows at Sharpe 1.4 / 2.0) inside the physical cross account requires scaling positions by $2.0 / 0.125 = 16\times$. Under [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L111) (`A6`) and [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L195) (`V11`), resting ladder orders reserve initial margin at `notional / leverage`, where `leverage` is capped at each coin's `maxLeverage` ($10\times$ for SUI and BNB, [facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L97)). Reserving initial margin for a 5-level resting ladder plus 16 short hedge positions at $16\times$ sizing exceeds $100\%$ of equity, so `A6` **refuses the ladder orders**; conversely, running the physical cross account at non-liquidating vault sizing ($\sigma_{\text{DN}} \approx 12.5\%$) without a separate statistical betting multiplier gives $< 10^{-15}$ power to reach $W_t = 21$ in two years.
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** Hedge with a single liquid index proxy (e.g., 50/50 BTC/ETH perp leg, so each hedge order is $N_0 / 2 \ge \$10$) or compute the dollar-neutral benchmark return algebraically on the step return series while running the replica in its faithful cross account, and apply the predictable volatility-scaled Kelly bet $b / \hat{\sigma}_{t-1}$ in the confirmer's wealth process $W_t = W_{t-1}(1 + \lambda_t R_t)$ rather than forcing $16\times$ physical leverage into the venue's initial-margin check.

#### 7. Six specification gaps block writing Lean/OCaml without guessing, and the twelve ★ laws miss funding (`A9`), slot/cycle bounds (`A5`, `R1`), and fill progress (`A7`)
- **Outcome changed:** Judged score and result (divergent Lean/OCaml implementations satisfy all 12 ★ laws while changing fills, funding, and window outputs).
- **The claim:** [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L3) and [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L248-L278) (`B3`) claim that Part A is the complete contract from which the Lean specification and OCaml implementation are written, and that the twelve laws marked ★ cover everything a judged number depends on.
- **Concrete construction:**
  1. **Sub-cadence bar inputs poison windows to permanent `Undef` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L49-L52), A4):** `A4` allows any whole-second `tick >= 1` and builds each tick's bar input from quote mids in the tick interval just closed. After 2026-06-21 (5.4 s snapshot cadence) or on the forward recorders (30 s cadence), a program with `tick = 1` s or `5` s has zero quotes in most tick intervals, yielding `Undef` bars ([facts-kernel-v2.md](audit/program/facts-kernel-v2.md#L113)). Under the stage 6 evaluator ([SEMANTICS.md](audit/program/SEMANTICS.md#L184)), a single `Undef` in the last $n$ ticks forces `mean`, `sum`, `std`, `min_over`, and `max_over` to output `Undef` for the entire window $n$, permanently disabling any bar-window expression. `A4` also fails to define bar `volume` on book venues (quotes have no volume).
  2. **Simultaneous order admission and book-depth depletion are unspecified ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L111), A6; [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L118-L125), A7; [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L261), `L4 ★`):**
     - At $\tau + \lambda$, when multiple slots/coins/legs place orders simultaneously and total initial margin exceeds free margin, `A6` does not specify whether admission is all-or-nothing or sequential in `(leg, coin, slot)` order, nor whether a resting limit order's initial margin is valued at its limit price $p$ or the coin's adverse mark.
     - At quote $t$, when two slots or two legs have orders arriving (taker) or resting (maker) on the same coin, `L4 ★` states per fill that "A taker fill is at recorded levels' prices... taking no more than each level shows", without depleting the snapshot's level sizes across simultaneous fills—allowing two slots to take $2\times$ the book's depth while satisfying `L4 ★`.
     - If a leg's position is partially reduced by Slot A while Slot B has a resting `reduce_only` order of the old larger size, `A7` ("Neither rule fills in part") and `L4 ★` ("A `reduce_only` order never... flips it") conflict when Slot B is reached.
  3. **Bitemporal funding timestamps and horizon ticks in `Chunk` ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L52), A4; [kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L225), B1):** `A4` states that a funding row's `knownAt` uses version 1's rule ($\max(\lfloor\text{reportedTime}\rfloor_{\text{hour}} + \delta, \text{reportedTime} + 60\text{ s})$, [SEMANTICS.md](audit/program/SEMANTICS.md#L100)), while `A9` settles funding at event time $t$. Yet `Chunk` (`B1`) gives each event only one `time` field, so Lean cannot distinguish settlement time $t$ from `knownAt`. Nor does `start`/`finish` specify the run's `[w_start, w_end)` horizon for generating clock ticks (`class 4`, not in the market) before the first event or after a trailing gap.
  4. **What the 12 ★ laws miss ([kernel-v2.md](audit/program/kernel-v2.md#L248-L278), B3):**
     - **No ★ law on Funding (`A9`):** An implementation that sets every funding payment to `0` and never aborts on `R_SETTLEMENT_MISSING` satisfies `A1 ★` (conservation holds with `funding = 0`), `A3 ★` (0 rounding), and all other 10 ★ laws.
     - **No ★ law on Cycles, `filled(slot)`, or Reconciliation (`A5`, `R1`):** `R1` is not ★, and `L4 ★` bounds fills per *order*, not per *slot* over a cycle (`filled(slot) <= size`); an engine that resets `filled(slot)` on every order replacement refills the same ladder level repeatedly within one cycle while satisfying all 12 ★ laws.
     - **`L4 ★`, `F1 ★`, `F2 ★` have no completeness/progress clause:** An engine that **never fills any order** (0 maker and 0 taker fills on every market) satisfies `L4 ★`, `F1 ★`, and `F2 ★` vacuously.
- **Smallest design change (hypothesis):** Carry forward the last known quote mid when building sub-cadence bar inputs in `A4` (and mark `volume` as `E_UNKNOWN_INPUT` on book venues unless trade events are present); specify sequential `(leg, coin, slot)` admission and book-level depletion across orders at the same quote (capping a resting `reduce_only` order's remainder to `|pos|` before matching); pass `(eventTime, knownAt)` in `Chunk` and `[w_start, w_end)` in `start`; and add ★ laws for `A9` (funding formula and `R_SETTLEMENT_MISSING`), `R1` + cycle invariants (`filled(slot) <= size` within a flat-to-flat cycle), and exact iff fill characterization in `L4 ★`.
